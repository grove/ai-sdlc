# Sources of measurement and control

The sources support the measurement and control principles in the catalog. They are not proof of effectiveness for each individual idea. Local examples, workflows and selection proposals are concrete proposals for testing, and the results must be documented in the project's own context. The sources were verified on 18 November 2026.

<a id="source-1"></a>

## [1] DORA: DORA's software delivery performance metrics

Used for delivery targets, context and balanced assessment of throughput and instability.

[Open the primary source](https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics/)

<a id="source-2"></a>

## [2] Forsgren and co-authors: The SPACE of Developer Productivity 2021

Used because developer productivity must be assessed through several dimensions.

[Open the primary source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-space-of-developer-productivity-theres-more-to-it-than-you-think/)

<a id="source-3"></a>

## [3] NIST: Secure Software Development Framework SP 800 218 Version 1.1 2022

Used for secure development as a practice through SDLC and work with causes of vulnerabilities.

[Open the primary source](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final)

<a id="source-4"></a>

## [4] NIST: AI Risk Management Framework Generative AI Profile 600 1 2024

Used as a basis for risk assessment in the development, use and evaluation of generative AI.

[Open the primary source](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence)

<a id="source-5"></a>

## [5] OWASP: Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025

Used for known risk categories such as instruction injection, sensitive information, output handling, and excessive leverage.

[Open the primary source](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/)

<a id="source-6"></a>

## [6] Google SRE: The Site Reliability Workbook Implementing SLOs

Used for service targets as a basis for prioritization and observation of reliability.

[Open the primary source](https://sre.google/workbook/implementing-slos/)

<a id="source-7"></a>

## [7] W3C: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2

Used for testable accessibility criteria and the need to take into account different user needs.

[Open the primary source](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)
