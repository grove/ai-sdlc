# Observation

Investigate what a change actually leads to in use, delivery flow, finances and operations. AI helps with compilation and explanation; numbers are calculated from traceable data with controlled analysis tools.

**The central question:** What changed in practice and how confident are we in the findings?

**Typical delivery:** A source-based analysis package with outcomes, cost, quality limits and limitations.

## Place in the learning loop

When observing, correct exposure, complete data and a credible comparison are essential. Use AI to find questions and explain validated analyses, while the calculations are done in verifiable tools. The analysis must show limitations before the team draws the learning conclusion.

Previous step is [Production](production.md). The next step is [Learning](learning.md). The loop is repeated when the experience provides the supporting evidence for a new improvement.
