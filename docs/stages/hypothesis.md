# Hypothesis

Design feasible improvement proposals and experiments that can show both benefits, costs and unwanted consequences.

**The question in this step:** What do we expect the change to improve?

**Typical delivery:** A testable hypothesis with alternative, measurement plan, risk limits and decision rule.

## Place in the learning loop

Hypothesis designs an experiment; the other steps implement and evaluate it. Experiments with autonomy, division, context and control must have agreed limits and measurements before they are started.

Previous step is [Problem](problem.md). The next step is [Context](context.md). The loop is repeated when the experience provides the supporting evidence for a new improvement.
