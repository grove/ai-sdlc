# P11 Investigating queues and friction in code review

<a id="p11"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Bottlenecks and workflow](../stages/problem.md#p-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Legacy Systems, Platform Engineering, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can analyze review processes to distinguish between waiting for a first response, many rounds of corrections and a lack of professional ownership. It can summarize recurring themes in the comments, so that the team chooses the right problem before measures against the review time are introduced.

## Inputs and Evidence

PR history, review times, comment threads, change size, code ownership and the approval rules that applied in the relevant period.

## Prerequisites

Legal access to PR data, understandable status definitions and team agreement on aggregate use of analysis data.

## Example and Deliverable

Long processing time is mainly due to missing domain information in the PR descriptions. AI delivers a findings note with examples and distribution of waiting and correction for the team.

## Safeguards and Review

Developers control a selection; consider the workflow as a whole and avoid ranking or monitoring individual reviewers.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to identified review problem, together with actual review time, repeated rounds and errors after approval.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
