# R28 Make shutdown and limited operation feasible

<a id="r28"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Controlled error handling and termination](../stages/production.md#r-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, AI in the product, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can create an operational setup to disable a risky feature or offer a simpler service without a full refund. It describes affected users, controls and reopening conditions. This makes interruption a planned course of action if the activation shows an unacceptable consequence.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved emergency stops, function flags, fallback, consequence maps, authorization roles and criteria for disabling or re-enabling a function.

## Prerequisites

Fallback and emergency stop must be actually implemented and tested, with named roles that can authorize the measures in the right situation.

## Example and Deliverable

A new recommendation function receives a stop instruction that switches to an approved standard display and documents which control signals and decisions are required before new activation.

## Safeguards and Review

Suspension and reopening follow pre-approved rules and authorizations. The model cannot itself change thresholds or activate the function again after a break.

## Measuring Impact

Time for controlled limitation of damage, along with accidental loss of other functions and premature or unauthorized re-opening.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
