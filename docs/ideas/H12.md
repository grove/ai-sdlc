# H12 Design a step-by-step introduction with a basis for comparison

<a id="h12"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Experiments and measurement baseline](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Product Development, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can design a plan where teams or services introduce the measure at different times, and propose relevant comparisons. It can register expected differences and simultaneous changes, so that the project examines the effect when random allocation cannot be carried out.

## Inputs and Evidence

Team history, work mix, planned changes, baseline windows and information on how working methods or tools can spread between teams.

## Prerequisites

Several comparable teams or periods, documented history and coordinated introduction with stable local measurement definitions.

## Example and Deliverable

Three teams introduce the same context template at different times. AI creates a rollout and analysis plan with previous periods, comparisons and registration of other process changes.

## Safeguards and Review

The method manager assesses comparability and trend assumptions; a difference between teams is not automatically an effect of the AI ​​measure.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to feasible design, along with data coverage, concurrent changes and the uncertainty of the estimated outcome.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
