# P14 Discover unstable requirements and unclear scope

<a id="p14"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Bottlenecks and workflow](../stages/problem.md#p-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Data and Analytics, Packaged Software, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compare versions of requirements, agreements and tasks to show which assumptions change after start-up. It can separate new needs from clarification of previous ambiguities and make the cost of unstable scope visible to decision-makers.

## Inputs and Evidence

Versioned requirements, change log, decisions, task history and consequence descriptions from teams and customers over time.

## Prerequisites

Access to version history, common understanding of requirement status and explicit link between changes and decisions.

## Example and Deliverable

A report order changes data definition several times during construction. AI creates a timeline of clarifications, decision sources and work that had to be redone.

## Safeguards and Review

Orderer and team approve the interpretation; necessary learning should not automatically be classified as poor requirements management or scope failure.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to clarify scope and changes after start-up, together with user outcomes and necessary learning space.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
