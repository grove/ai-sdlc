# V20 Test source basis and waiver in generative responses

<a id="v20"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Specialized deliveries](../stages/verification.md#v-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Documents and Decisions, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can help evaluate whether generative responses are supported by stated sources and address missing knowledge. It may propose questions without a basis for answers and conflicting documents. This reveals weaknesses that are not captured by an assessment of whether the answer seems well-formulated.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved set of questions, source extracts, conclusion or professional assessment criteria, expected deviations and saved answers from the solution.

## Prerequisites

The team needs a versioned evaluation set and clear rules for source use, uncertainty and which questions the solution must refrain from.

## Example and Deliverable

An internal supervisor is tested on expired guidelines and questions without coverage; AI supplies an assessment list of unsubstantiated claims and missing reservations.

## Safeguards and Review

Subject experts check representative answers and critical cases. AI as a judge is calibrated against human judgments and never provides a single proof of correctness.

## Measuring Impact

Proportion of expertly supported answers and correct refusals, together with assessment time and incorrect rejections of questions that can actually be answered.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
