# K07 Provide the developer with relevant operational context

<a id="k07"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [System and domain](../stages/context.md#k-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Platform Engineering, Legacy Systems

## Opportunity

AI can summarize previous events, operating limits, capacity problems and recurring failure patterns for the area to be changed. In this way, the team can make operational considerations concrete before execution and avoid repeating known problems in new deliveries.

## Inputs and Evidence

Event reports, operational targets, runbooks, system limits and aggregated telemetry from affected service.

## Prerequisites

Available event data with known quality and rights that limit visibility to the needs of the task.

## Example and Deliverable

Before a cache change, the team receives a short operating note about previous failures at expiration, known load peaks and the alarms that must be taken into account.

## Safeguards and Review

Service owner validates causal explanations; logs are cleaned, and missing events or incomplete telemetry are reported.

## Measuring Impact

Clarification time and later operational-related rework, supplemented by the quality of source references and repeated incident types.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
