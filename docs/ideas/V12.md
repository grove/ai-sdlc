# V12 Check configuration and infrastructure against requirements

<a id="v12"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Safety and robustness](../stages/verification.md#v-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can interpret planned changes in infrastructure and configuration against safety rules and operational requirements. It explains differences such as open ports, weak rights or unexpected resource changes. This can reduce the time professionals spend understanding large plan printouts before the change is approved.

## Inputs and Evidence

Infrastructure plan, configuration diff, network rules, identity policy, approved base profiles and results from deterministic policy checks.

## Prerequisites

The plan and policy must be available without secrets, and there must be a responsible approver for resource changes.

## Example and Deliverable

Before rolling out a storage service, AI makes a review that points to public availability, lack of encryption options and wider rights than the service needs.

## Safeguards and Review

The platform manager determines acceptance. Deterministic policy gates should still block forbidden changes, and secrets should not be part of the model's context.

## Measuring Impact

Time to approved infrastructure review, along with policy violations that slip through and production failures associated with the configuration.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
