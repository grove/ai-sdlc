# L03 Improve the explanation of why something works

<a id="l03"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Improve hypotheses and prioritization](../stages/learning.md#l-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Data and Analytics, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can connect process observations, user outcomes and qualitative experiences to an explicit explanation of the change. It identifies which links have evidence, and which are only assumed, so that the next experiment can test the mechanism more precisely.

## Inputs and Evidence

Results, working samples, control procedures, user input and documented alternative explanations.

## Prerequisites

More types of evidence and a willingness to test other explanations than the use of AI alone.

## Example and Deliverable

Deliveries are faster after agent introduction. AI suggests that better acceptance criteria can explain parts of the effect and creates a testable follow-up hypothesis.

## Safeguards and Review

Analyst and team separate mechanism hypothesis from proven cause. Avoid post-rationalization that only fits the observed result.

## Measuring Impact

Time for a clarified critical assumption in the next attempt, with quality of decision and confirmed misinterpretations.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
