# L04 Select the next constraint in the delivery flow

<a id="l04"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Improve hypotheses and prioritization](../stages/learning.md#l-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Product Development, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can compile persistent queues, errors and resource use after an improvement measure and suggest the next bottleneck worth investigating. The proposals are linked to the value stream and to who can actually influence the limitation, so that learning becomes a concrete priority.

## Inputs and Evidence

Validated flow data, results from previous measures, dependencies, risks and product priorities.

## Prerequisites

Updated flowchart and ownership of changes across relevant roles.

## Example and Deliverable

Code production has become faster. AI creates a problem card for test environment waiting with source evidence, affected work and a proposal for a limited experiment.

## Safeguards and Review

Value stream owner prioritizes product targets and available capacity. Do not optimize a visible local goal without improving the overall value stream.

## Measuring Impact

Reduced waiting time at the selected bottleneck and total time to value, with quality and total work effort.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
