# H24 Investigate a better distribution of verification work

<a id="h24"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Economy capacity and working methods](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Regulated Systems, Embedded and OT, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can help the team formulate hypotheses about which checks should come earlier, be automated or supplemented. It can compare work arrangements with the same quality requirements, so that the experiment investigates less waiting and double work without reducing the necessary verification.

## Inputs and Evidence

Test strategy, review routines, discovery history, required controls and time spent divided between automatic testing, manual review and correction.

## Prerequisites

Known control requirements, understanding of previous errors and responsible professionals who can assess the strength of the verification.

## Example and Deliverable

Requirements control is moved to before implementation, while security review is retained. AI creates an experimental plan that measures whether fewer misunderstandings reduce later review and rework.

## Safeguards and Review

The test and safety officer approves the control system; previous green status or several AI reviewers are not proof of sufficient quality.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to verified verification hypothesis, along with control time, findings, errors after delivery and the independence of the proofs.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
