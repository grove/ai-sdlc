# H09 Choosing main goals and balancing quality goals

<a id="h09"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Experiments and measurement baseline](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Maintenance, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can link the improvement hypothesis to a limited set of goals that cover the desired result and possible side effects. It can suggest which indicators show value, and which only describe activity, so that the experiment avoids one-sided optimization.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hypothesis, impact chain, quality requirements, cost framework and available measurement sources in product, development flow, operation and the team's working environment.

## Prerequisites

A clarified hypothesis, known quality requirements and insight into which data the team can collect reliably.

## Example and Deliverable

An agent for test writing gets time for an approved test package as the main goal. AI also proposes discovery rate, review time and errors after delivery as control targets.

## Safeguards and Review

The mortgage owner and the quality manager approve the targets; do not use more manufactured tests or more AI usage as a sufficient indicator of value.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to approved target set, together with translated side effects, measurement load and actual decision benefit after the trial.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
