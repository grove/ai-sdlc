# O19 Explain changes in service quality

<a id="o19"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Operational quality and risk](../stages/observation.md#o-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Product Development, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can summarize the development in SLO, error budget, response time and successful user operations per service and cohort. It links technical measures to user history, and suggests investigations when an average hides increasing tail latency or degradation in some regions.

## Inputs and Evidence

Agreed SLI and SLO, latency distributions, events, load and release or exposure marks.

## Prerequisites

Meaningful service goals and instrumentation of actual user operations.

## Example and Deliverable

The average time is unchanged, but p95 rises. AI creates an operational analysis that shows affected operations and possible simultaneous changes.

## Safeguards and Review

Service owner validates measurement expression and interpretation. AI must not move the target or change the denominator to get green status.

## Measuring Impact

SLO fulfillment and relevant latency percentiles, with measurement data quality and number of affected users.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
