# K23 Gather frameworks for dependencies and license usage

<a id="k23"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Sources of data and work surface](../stages/context.md#k-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Packaged Software, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can retrieve technical and organizational frameworks for libraries, components and reused examples. It can clearly inform the execution of approved versions, known compatibility limits and conditions that require professional or legal clarification.

## Inputs and Evidence

Dependency register, approval lists, license policy, supplier requirements and current build files.

## Prerequisites

An identifiable dependency basis and owners for approval of new components and terms of use.

## Example and Deliverable

Before a PDF function is built, AI creates a compilation of permitted libraries, supported platforms and unresolved license issues for the relevant options.

## Safeguards and Review

SCA tool and legal or technical owner confirm facts; AI must not invent packages or give binding license approval.

## Measuring Impact

Time for clarified component selection, balanced with later license deviations, unwanted dependencies and compatibility errors.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
