# R15 Plan firmware rollout by device groups

<a id="r15"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Deployment and activation](../stages/production.md#r-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Embedded and OT, Regulated Systems, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can divide the device fleet into relevant groups according to hardware, software version, connection and error consequence. It suggests sequence and checkpoints for approved update. This supports safer deployment when devices have different limitations, and a bug update cannot necessarily be fixed immediately from a distance.

## Inputs and Evidence

Device registry, hardware variants, battery and network requirements, signed packages, compatibility matrix and documented device recoveries.

## Prerequisites

Device identity and compatibility must be reliably known, with a tested update mechanism and plan for devices that lose connectivity.

## Example and Deliverable

For a sensor park, AI creates a rollout plan where compatible and available devices are updated first, while insufficient power and unknown hardware block activation.

## Safeguards and Review

The subject manager approves cohorts and update conditions. The devices' secure update mechanisms enforce signature and compatibility, and AI cannot override security boundaries.

## Measuring Impact

Labor time per controlled device rollout, along with failed updates, unavailable devices, and need for physical recovery after the update.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
