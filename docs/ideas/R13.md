# R13 Coordinate publishing of interfaces and consumer transitions

<a id="r13"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Deployment and activation](../stages/production.md#r-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Platform Engineering, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can create a concrete activation plan for new API or event versions and follow up on necessary confirmations from consumer owners. It distinguishes availability from decommissioning of previous interfaces. This makes delivery easier to coordinate when other teams or external partners have to change their own solutions.

## Inputs and Evidence

Publication plan, approved contracts, consumer register, retirement deadlines, transition guidance and agreed contact and approval processes for integration partners.

## Prerequisites

The consumers must be able to be identified reasonably well, and the contracts must state how new versions are published and old ones are phased out.

## Example and Deliverable

A new order API gets a publishing package with version endpoint, migration guide and consumer overview showing who has confirmed readiness for the transition.

## Safeguards and Review

Integration owner approves publication and communication. No old version is removed without agreed terms, and automatic messages require an explicit sending mandate.

## Measuring Impact

Time to agreed consumer transition, together with clients that are broken, unanswered transition clarifications and extensions of the previous interface's lifetime.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
