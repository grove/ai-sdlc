# P32 Write a verifiable problem card

<a id="p32"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Costs, risk and prioritization](../stages/problem.md#p-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Maintenance, Documents and Decisions, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can transform quality-assured findings into a compact problem card with affected actor, observable situation, consequence and delimitation. It can separate facts from assumptions and mark a missing baseline, so that the next step starts with a clear and testable understanding of the problem.

## Inputs and Evidence

Confirmed findings, source references, responsible owner, current situation, available measurements and the project's limitation in time and scope.

## Prerequisites

Agreed template, source coverage for key statements and a named person who owns the problem definition.

## Example and Deliverable

The analysis shows waiting for test data. AI creates a problem card with the start and end of the process, the tasks involved, data bases and unresolved questions.

## Safeguards and Review

The issue's owner approves the card and sources; AI does not fill in blanks with plausible but undocumented baseline values.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to approved problem card, together with missing sources, later rounds of clarification and misunderstanding of scope.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
