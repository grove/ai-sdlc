# P25 Make the cost of today's problem visible

<a id="p25"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Costs, risk and prioritization](../stages/problem.md#p-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Packaged Software, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can build a simple cost model from documented time trials, events and resource prices. It can distinguish between actual payment, releaseable capacity and uncertain consequences, so that the project owner understands the order of magnitude and which assumptions must be examined before prioritization.

## Inputs and Evidence

Representative time measurements, case volume, invoiced costs, internal calculation rates and documented user or operational consequences from the problem.

## Prerequisites

Clarified calculation principles, representative work samples and access to the finance manager who can assess the assumptions in the estimate.

## Example and Deliverable

Manual reconciliation requires fixed hours each month and causes delays. AI delivers a cost estimate with calculations, intervals, sources and explicit assumptions.

## Safeguards and Review

Finance and process owner approves assumptions; time value and the value of the same deliveries shall not be counted twice.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to a verifiable cost base, together with calculation errors and deviations from later observed actual costs.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
