# O32 Create a verifiable basis for decision-making

<a id="o32"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Credible impact analysis](../stages/observation.md#o-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Regulated Systems, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can collect the observations in one structured analysis package that shows hypothesis, basis for measurement, outcome, cost, quality limits and unanswered questions. It links claims to sources and marks data gaps, so that the learning step has a controlled basis for choosing further action.

## Inputs and Evidence

Pre-determined decision rules, validated analyses, deviations, cost accounting and qualitative observations.

## Prerequisites

A hypothesis card and an analysis package with traceability to all significant claims.

## Example and Deliverable

A pilot trial has been completed. AI prepares a decision package with supported findings, conflicting evidence and a clear assessment of whether more data is needed.

## Safeguards and Review

Decision owner controls sources and thresholds. AI should show negative findings and missing data, not write a convincing sales story.

## Measuring Impact

Time for a quality-controlled analysis package, with errors in the analysis, source link and completeness of the evidence.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
