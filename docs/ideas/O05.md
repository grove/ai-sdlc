# O05 Follow changes in support needs

<a id="o05"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Users and business outcomes](../stages/observation.md#o-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Maintenance, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can group inquiries by release and separate training needs, errors, unclear language and lack of functionality. This way, the team can see if faster development shifts work to customer support or operations, and if support content actually relieves users.

## Inputs and Evidence

De-identified support cases, product version, active users, response times and a controlled category overview.

## Prerequisites

Link between inquiry and version as well as access to representative support content.

## Example and Deliverable

A new dashboard provides more questions. AI makes a weekly overview of confirmed errors, comprehension problems and documentation gaps per release.

## Safeguards and Review

The support manager checks categorization with a selection. Take into account changes in the number of users and avoid automatic closing of cases.

## Measuring Impact

Inquiries and resolution time per relevant user task, with customer satisfaction and error classification as control measures.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
