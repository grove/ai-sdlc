# O08 Link product goals to observed results

<a id="o08"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Users and business outcomes](../stages/observation.md#o-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Packaged Software, Data and Analytics

## Opportunity

AI can follow a pre-registered value hypothesis from expected user change to measured business outcome. It collects underlying data, highlights uncovered assumptions and describes the gap between technical delivery, use and value without pretending that causality has already been proven.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hypothesis card, target definitions, product data, costs, control basis and documented external events.

## Prerequisites

An explicit product goal and a measurable chain from function to desired outcome.

## Example and Deliverable

A project must reduce wrong orders. AI delivers a benefit report that separates changed use, corrected orders and other simultaneous measures.

## Safeguards and Review

Product owner and analysis manager approve the value chain and alternative explanations. AI shall not book hypothetical benefits as realised.

## Measuring Impact

Agreed business outcome per comparable unit, with impact uncertainty and unwanted consequences clearly reported.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
