# H20 Include error consequence in the improvement hypothesis

<a id="h20"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Economy capacity and working methods](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Regulated Systems, Embedded and OT, Data and Analytics, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can structure possible failure outcomes and highlight how probability, severity and recovery affect the usefulness of a measure. It can help the team plan necessary quality limits, so that time savings are not considered in isolation from costly error correction or unacceptable damage.

## Inputs and Evidence

Risk assessment, historical events, rollback costs, affected users and the company's limits on which error consequences can be accepted.

## Prerequisites

Named risk owner, access to relevant fault history and clear requirements that cannot be exchanged for time gain.

## Example and Deliverable

A migration agent saves manual work, but incorrect mapping can cause major fixes. AI creates scenarios and control points that form part of the trial decision.

## Safeguards and Review

Risk owner sets acceptance limits; serious security requirements cannot be offset by the expected financial gain in a simple calculation.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to complete risk hypothesis, together with translated error outcomes, control cost and compliance with agreed quality limits.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
