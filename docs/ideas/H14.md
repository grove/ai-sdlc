# H14 Plan measurement window and delayed effects

<a id="h14"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Experiments and measurement baseline](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Data and Analytics, Packaged Software, Maintenance, Embedded and OT

## Opportunity

AI can propose observation windows that cover learning, normal operation and later consequences of a change. It can highlight the difference between rapid local time gain and effects that only become apparent during maintenance or actual use, so that the trial is not terminated prematurely.

## Inputs and Evidence

Work rhythm, release frequency, season, training schedule and knowledge of when quality defects, maintenance needs and product failures usually become visible.

## Prerequisites

Understanding of delivery and usage cycles, known training needs and responsibility for follow-up after the first delivery.

## Example and Deliverable

AI supports a migration with monthly runs. AI creates a measurement plan that covers start-up, first normal driving and follow-up work before profit is assessed.

## Safeguards and Review

The service and analytics manager approves the window; avoid choosing an end date only after the yield curve looks favorable.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to suitable observation schedule, together with undetected delayed errors, seasonal effects and the cost of follow-up.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
