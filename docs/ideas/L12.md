# L12 Turn incident learning into preventive measures

<a id="l12"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Maintain knowledge and competence](../stages/learning.md#l-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Integrations, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can summarize approved incident analyzes and propose specific changes in test, observation, context or workflow. It follows the link from confirmed contributory relationship to an action that can be tried, and helps the team to close learning that otherwise remains in minutes.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved incident analyses, timelines, relevant tests, configuration and recorded improvement measures.

## Prerequisites

A completed incident analysis and authority to change the affected work mechanisms.

## Example and Deliverable

A data migration produced duplicates. AI proposes an idempotence test and a control point in the migration recipe, with a link to the evidence of the event.

## Safeguards and Review

The incident owner validates the measures and places responsibility without a blame focus. Verify that the measure affects the cause rather than just the symptom.

## Measuring Impact

Proportion of measures with verified effect and repetition rate, with measure cost and new side effects.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
