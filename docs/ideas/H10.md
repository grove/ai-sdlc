# H10 Define repeatable measurements

<a id="h10"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Experiments and measurement baseline](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Data and Analytics, Product Development, Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can prepare operational definitions for goals, including numerator, denominator, start, end and exclusions. It can find ambiguous terms and different data sources, so that the baseline and trial period measure the same thing and the result can be verified by others.

## Inputs and Evidence

Selected indicators, data sources' fields, working definitions, time stamps and examples of normal, interrupted and reopened cases in the project.

## Prerequisites

Access to the data form, ownership of the indicators and example cases that cover important exceptions in the workflow.

## Example and Deliverable

"Finished task" means different things in two teams. AI creates a metric definition with acceptance point, work committee and handling of reopened cases before comparison.

## Safeguards and Review

Data owner checks the definitions against actual fields and test examples; easily accessible timestamps should not replace the relevant term without explanation.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to reproducible definition, along with missing data, interpretation differences and changes in definition during the trial.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
