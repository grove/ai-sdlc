# O28 Discover which task types the measure helps

<a id="o28"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Credible impact analysis](../stages/observation.md#o-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Legacy Systems, Product Development, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can summarize results for pre-defined categories such as small bug fixes, new integrations and complex migrations. The analysis makes it possible to limit the use to suitable work, rather than allowing an overall average to hide both good and bad experiences.

## Inputs and Evidence

Task type, complexity indicators, risk, agent use and comparable measurements of time, cost and quality.

## Prerequisites

A task taxonomy chosen before the analysis and consistent recording of work effort.

## Example and Deliverable

An agent helps with standard changes, but creates extra work in legacy code. AI creates a profile report with recommended questions before further use.

## Safeguards and Review

Team and analyst control small samples and many comparisons. Do not select segments by result without exploratory labeling.

## Measuring Impact

Net time or cost per task category, with quality outcome, uncertainty and coverage in the selection.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
