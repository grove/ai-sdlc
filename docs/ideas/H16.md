# H16 Plan traceable data collection for the trial

<a id="h16"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Experiments and measurement baseline](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Data and Analytics, Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems, AI in the product

## Opportunity

AI can suggest which events and identifiers are needed to connect measures, work, verification, delivery and results. It can create a minimal instrumentation plan, so that later analysis can verify the effect without collecting unnecessarily detailed or sensitive information.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hypothesis, measurement definitions, existing system integrations, data classes and identifiers for tasks, agent runs, changes and deliverables in the project.

## Prerequisites

Known data sources, stable identifiers and owners who can approve metadata, access and required storage time.

## Example and Deliverable

A pilot lacks a link between agent use and approved deliveries. AI creates an event plan with few metadata fields, owners, storage time and control of missing links.

## Safeguards and Review

The Data and Privacy Officer approves needs and access; raw prompt content or person activity is not saved simply because it is possible.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to usable measurement plan, together with degree of coupling, data minimization and cost of collection and maintenance.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
