# H04 Formulate counter-hypotheses and falsifying observations

<a id="h04"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Alternatives and mode of operation](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Data and Analytics, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can challenge a proposed solution by formulating alternative explanations and observations that would speak against the hypothesis. It can help the team plan questions and measurements that can also disprove the favorite solution, rather than just confirm it.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hypothesis, justification, baseline, relevant experience and documented assumptions behind the chosen working method or solution in the project.

## Prerequisites

Access to the reasoning behind the measure and a team environment where negative findings can influence the decision.

## Example and Deliverable

Faster code production can result in more review work without faster delivery. AI creates a counter-hypothesis and proposes measuring total time, queues and rework.

## Safeguards and Review

The supervisor distinguishes plausible alternatives from constructed counter-arguments; neither AI nor hypothesis author shall determine the outcome without data.

## Measuring Impact

Measure the proportion of hypotheses with rejection criteria, together with relevant alternative explanations and the cost of unnecessary extra measurements.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
