# K01 Map the code base and system architecture

<a id="k01"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [System and domain](../stages/context.md#k-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Legacy Systems, Platform Engineering, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can read selected repositories, build files and service descriptions and propose a map of components, responsibilities and technical dependencies. The map gives the team a common starting point and makes it easier to find where a change belongs.

## Inputs and Evidence

Source code on known revision, build files, service registry, architecture descriptions and relevant owners.

## Prerequisites

Read access to necessary repositories and a professional who knows the system's most important operating conditions.

## Example and Deliverable

In an older ordering system, AI creates a component map showing entry points, databases and message queues, with links to the files that support the connections.

## Safeguards and Review

Technical owner controls critical connections; statically derived conditions are marked and distinguished from documented or observed driving conditions.

## Measuring Impact

Time to localized area of change, supplemented by proportion of incorrect dependencies and discovered knowledge gaps.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
