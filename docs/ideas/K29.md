# K29 Find previous similar work

<a id="k29"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Maintenance and reuse of context](../stages/context.md#k-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Legacy Systems, Integrations, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can search approved tasks, changes and decisions for work that is similar to the task in question. It can suggest reusable patterns and explain the differences, giving the team a starting point without uncritically copying previous solutions.

## Inputs and Evidence

Task description, historical changes, documented outcomes, source versions and access limits.

## Prerequisites

Searchable historical deliveries and information about version and outcome that make the comparison meaningful.

## Example and Deliverable

Before a new event consumer is built, AI finds three previous implementations and delivers a comparison of error handling, data requirements and which parts can be reused.

## Safeguards and Review

Developer checks relevance, quality and applicable requirements; historical approval does not mean that the solution is still suitable.

## Measuring Impact

Time spent on research and startup, balanced with errors from inappropriate reuse and translating differences.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
