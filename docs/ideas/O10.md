# O10 Detect when the bottleneck moves

<a id="o10"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Delivery flow and resource use](../stages/observation.md#o-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can follow queues, work in progress and blockages after a way of working changes. It suggests where the capacity is now limited, so that the team does not continue to optimize code production when the test environment, review or dependencies have become the new limitation.

## Inputs and Evidence

State transitions, queue length, classes of service, capacity events and blocking reasons at the team level.

## Prerequisites

Stable work statuses and recording of why cases are at a standstill.

## Example and Deliverable

An agent increases the number of amendments. AI shows a growing review queue and makes a proposal for observations that can confirm the capacity problem.

## Safeguards and Review

The team examines task mix and concurrent absences. Covariation alone does not determine which measure should be chosen.

## Measuring Impact

Waiting time at the relevant bottleneck and total throughput, with age of unfinished work and quality.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
