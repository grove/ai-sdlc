# V05 Investigate concurrency and state-dependent errors

<a id="v05"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Requirements and functional correctness](../stages/verification.md#v-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Product Development, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can read sequences, latching and state transitions to suggest competing events that should be tested. It highlights assumptions about order, repeated messages and simultaneous use. The suggestions can make it easier to uncover rare errors before they affect real work processes.

## Inputs and Evidence

State model, transaction limits, message contracts, locking principles, timeouts and relevant events from previous error reports.

## Prerequisites

The system's desired consistency and idempotence must be clarified, and the test environment must support a controlled sequence of events.

## Example and Deliverable

For a reservation solution, AI creates a test plan where two clients book the last seat at the same time, and one message is delivered again after a timeout.

## Safeguards and Review

The specialist confirms consistency requirements and expected final results. The tests must provoke actual simultaneous processes, not just describe them in text.

## Measuring Impact

Confirmed concurrency bugs found before release, along with reproducibility rate and occurrence of duplicate or lost operations after release.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
