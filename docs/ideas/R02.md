# R02 Visible which services and users are affected

<a id="r02"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Preparation and release decision](../stages/production.md#r-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Integrations, Legacy Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compare a release with service maps, dependencies and client groups to describe the possible extent of impact. It can identify owners who should be involved and uncertain links that require clarification. This provides a more concrete basis for the choice of rollout and communication to affected recipients.

## Inputs and Evidence

The release's change list, dependency map, service owners, user groups, data sources and documented links between services and clients.

## Prerequisites

A maintained service map or equivalent sources must exist, with owners who can verify the most important dependencies.

## Example and Deliverable

A change in federated authentication gets a consequence map showing internal apps, external clients, and affected guard layers, including dependencies that need to be confirmed.

## Safeguards and Review

Service owner validates critical links. Incomplete mapping must be disclosed, and AI cannot assume that unknown dependencies mean that no one is affected.

## Measuring Impact

Time to clarify the scope of consequences, together with unexpected affected services and missing recipients when the release is implemented.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
