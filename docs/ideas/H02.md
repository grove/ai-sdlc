# H02 Describe how a measure is expected to produce an effect

<a id="h02"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Alternatives and mode of operation](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Maintenance, Data and Analytics

## Opportunity

AI can formulate an assumed impact chain from measures to changed work, delivery and user outcomes. It can mark where the connection is based on evidence or assumptions, so that the team knows which elements must be measured before a gain can be attributed to the measure.

## Inputs and Evidence

Problem, proposed measures, value stream map, existing measurements and known dependencies between work, delivery and desired effect.

## Prerequisites

Understanding of the entire delivery flow, clarified goals and professional support to assess alternative causal explanations.

## Example and Deliverable

A test agent should reduce repetitive work and free up capacity for error correction. AI creates an impact chain with measurement points and assumptions that must be verified.

## Safeguards and Review

The team approves the mechanism; the chain is a hypothesis and must not be presented as documented causation prior to investigation.

## Measuring Impact

Measure the time to a clear chain of effect, together with the number of unconfirmed links and subsequent deviations from the expected mechanism.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
