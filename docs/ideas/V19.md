# V19 Check whether a model has been tested on the right basis

<a id="v19"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Specialized deliveries](../stages/verification.md#v-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Data and Analytics, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can review machine learning evaluation setups and look for data leakage, unrepresentative samples and missing segments. It can suggest supplementary analyzes of uncertainty and error. This supports a more realistic assessment of expected performance before the model is put into an actual work process.

## Inputs and Evidence

Dataset descriptions, sharing rules, training history, evaluation code, segment results, and the intended use of the model in the product.

## Prerequisites

The model's area of use must be clarified, and the data set, code and splitting procedure must be traceable and accessible for control.

## Example and Deliverable

With a forecast model, AI points out that later customer events are included in the training fields, and delivers a proposal for timely validation and segment report.

## Safeguards and Review

The model manager checks data separation and results with statistical methods. AI does not assess alone whether the model is safe or suitable for the decision.

## Measuring Impact

Time for reliable evaluation, along with detected data leakage and the difference between test results and later results in use.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
