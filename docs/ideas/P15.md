# P15 Reveal waiting for environments and accesses

<a id="p15"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Bottlenecks and workflow](../stages/problem.md#p-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Data and Analytics, Integrations, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can collate orders of environments, test data and accesses to show repeated waiting patterns. It can investigate whether the wait is due to a lack of information, capacity or necessary controls, so that the platform team gets a concrete and defined improvement problem.

## Inputs and Evidence

Service requests, processing times, rejection reasons, environmental overview and applicable requirements for access and data approval in the business.

## Prerequisites

Access to order data, stable registration of returns and an overview of which controls are required.

## Example and Deliverable

Test environment orders are often returned because the test data owner is missing. AI delivers a problem card with documented return reasons, time spent and affected projects to the platform team.

## Safeguards and Review

Platform and security owner confirms necessary controls; the goal is less unnecessary waiting, not to weaken access requirements.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to identified waiting pattern, along with ordering time, return rate and unauthorized or erroneous accesses.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
