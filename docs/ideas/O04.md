# O04 Visualize the results of different user groups

<a id="o04"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Users and business outcomes](../stages/observation.md#o-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Mobile and Client Software, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can summarize the effect for predefined user groups, device types and accessibility needs. It can reveal that an improvement for the majority simultaneously creates more friction for certain groups, so that the team prioritizes follow-up on a more precise basis.

## Inputs and Evidence

Aggregated and legally processed segment data, user samples, aid findings and task success per device type.

## Prerequisites

Clarified groups, privacy-friendly data processing and participation from relevant users.

## Example and Deliverable

A mobile update works better in general. AI compiles screen reader tests and user samples into a report that points to a deteriorated confirmation dialog.

## Safeguards and Review

The accessibility officer and actual users validate findings. Do not infer sensitive characteristics from behavior or publish recognizable small groups.

## Measuring Impact

Task completion per relevant group and unit, with minimum sample size and confirmed availability errors.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
