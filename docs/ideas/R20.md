# R20 Prepare the support staff for expected questions

<a id="r20"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Operational readiness and reception](../stages/production.md#r-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Product Development, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can translate release information, known limitations and test findings into a support-specific knowledge package. It can propose questions, clarification steps and escalation rules that apply to the new solution. This helps support staff provide consistent answers on deployment without having to interpret developers' raw change history.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved release information, user history, known problems, error codes, support procedures and clarified boundary between first line and professional escalation.

## Prerequisites

The support channels must have owners and an approved knowledge base, with clear limits on what advice they can give.

## Example and Deliverable

For changed logins, AI creates a support sheet that separates the wrong client version, lack of access and service errors, with approved advice and necessary escalation information.

## Safeguards and Review

The support officer and subject owner approve the answers. Councils must not encourage the sharing of secrets, bypassing access controls or performing unauthorized actions.

## Measuring Impact

Time for the support apparatus to clear, along with incorrect default responses, unnecessary escalations and inquiries with no approved guidance available.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
