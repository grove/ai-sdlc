# P10 Map blocking dependencies between teams

<a id="p10"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Bottlenecks and workflow](../stages/problem.md#p-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Platform Engineering, Product Development, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can collect dependencies from cases, plans and approved meeting notes, and show which deliverables are blocking others. It can separate technical dependencies from decision-making and capacity obstacles, so that the project clarifies responsibility and the real extent of the problem.

## Inputs and Evidence

Linked tasks, dependency descriptions, ownership, planned deliveries and documented blocks from affected teams or suppliers.

## Prerequisites

Access to relevant project areas, stable identification of deliveries and clarified sharing between the teams.

## Example and Deliverable

Three teams are waiting for the same test interface without a common owner. AI creates a dependency map with documented blocks, contact roles and unconfirmed connections for review.

## Safeguards and Review

Affected teams confirm the links and who owns the issue; absence of documentation shall not be interpreted as absence of dependencies.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to clarified dependency and blocked days, along with faulty links and coordination efforts.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
