# L05 Clear and prioritize the improvement queue

<a id="l05"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Improve hypotheses and prioritization](../stages/learning.md#l-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can merge overlapping improvement proposals, clarify assumptions and help the team prioritize learning that can influence the next decision. It provides a reasoned overview of value, effort, risk and knowledge gaps rather than an apparently objective total score.

## Inputs and Evidence

Improvement proposals, learning items, product targets, cost estimates, risks and dependencies between measures.

## Prerequisites

Common prioritization criteria and an overview of capacity for both implementation and evaluation.

## Example and Deliverable

The team has many AI ideas. AI creates a prioritized experiment queue with justifications, dependencies and which uncertain assumptions each experiment can clarify.

## Safeguards and Review

The product owner and team determine the priority. Numerical scores must have explained assumptions and cannot override safety requirements.

## Measuring Impact

Time to select and start a relevant experiment, with completed learning loops and documented benefit.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
