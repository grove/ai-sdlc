# P21 Describe data quality issues with actual consequence

<a id="p21"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Technology data and operation](../stages/problem.md#p-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Data and Analytics, Packaged Software, Integrations, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compile data controls and professional complaints to show how errors in data affect decisions or processes. It can link blank fields, duplicates and unclear terms to affected deliverables, so that data issues have a clear owner and value.

## Inputs and Evidence

Data profiles, quality tests, term catalog, reconciliation findings and descriptions of the decisions or processes that use the relevant data.

## Prerequisites

Metadata or approved data samples, documented quality rules and named owners of data and concepts.

## Example and Deliverable

Different definitions of active customer give conflicting reports. AI creates a problem note that links definitions, sources, reports and decision consequences together for review.

## Safeguards and Review

Data owner and subject manager validate definitions and consistency; raw personal data should not be shared just to explain the problem.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to confirmed data issue, along with reconciliation errors, erroneous quality findings and affected business decisions over time.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
