# R16 Transfer approved configuration between environments

<a id="r16"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Deployment and activation](../stages/production.md#r-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Packaged Software, Integrations, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can compare an approved configuration package with the characteristics of the target environment and explain necessary environment-specific differences. It can create a controlled transfer plan for packaged solutions and low-code platforms. This reduces manual errors when publishing rules, workflows and rights that are not delivered as normal application code.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved configuration, export or changepack, environment profile, role setup, dependencies, and vendor-supported procedures for transferring between environments.

## Prerequisites

The configuration must be able to be exported or compared, and environmental differences must be known without giving the model unnecessary rights.

## Example and Deliverable

An approved procurement flow receives a publishing plan that preserves local endpoints and access groups, with pre- and post-checks for configuration differences in the target environment.

## Safeguards and Review

The platform and process owner approves the mapping. Secrets are kept outside the package, and changes are made through supported mechanisms with explicit access.

## Measuring Impact

Time to correct configuration transfer, along with environmental anomalies, wrongly assigned rights and the need for manual corrections after activation.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
