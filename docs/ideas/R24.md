# R24 Prepare controlled publication of models and data products

<a id="r24"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Operational readiness and reception](../stages/production.md#r-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Data and Analytics, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can collect model version, dataset dependencies, evaluation evidence and usage limits in a publishing package. It can check that routing and fallback are planned before activation. This supports the delivery of solutions where the result depends on both software, data and model configuration which must be traceable together.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved model or data product, registry identity, evaluation results, data contracts, routing rules, fallback and permitted area of use for receiving services.

## Prerequisites

Models and data must be versioned and approved, with explicit usage limits and a controllable mechanism for activation and rollback.

## Example and Deliverable

For a forecasting service, AI creates an activation package with model version, latest approved dataset, fallback to previous method and clear boundaries for which customers are covered.

## Safeguards and Review

Model and data owner approves publication. AI cannot expand the area of ​​use or change the model automatically without a new evaluation and express authorization.

## Measuring Impact

Time to traceable model activation, along with version errors, missing fallback and use outside the approved data or decision limits.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
