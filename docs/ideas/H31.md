# H31 Design learning when production is expensive or infrequent

<a id="h31"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Risk and decision rules](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Embedded and OT, Regulated Systems, Mobile and Client Software, Legacy Systems

## Opportunity

AI can propose a step-by-step investigation with historical tasks, simulation, test bench and controlled field test. It can distinguish what each level can actually show, so that the project reduces uncertainty when releases are rare or errors can have major consequences.

## Inputs and Evidence

Delivery cycle, security requirements, representative error situations, available test environments and known differences between simulation, laboratory testing and actual operation of the solution.

## Prerequisites

Available representative test environments, documented safety limits and professional ownership of the transitions between each level of the experiment.

## Example and Deliverable

A firmware change cannot be tested directly in the field. AI creates an evidence plan from historical test case via test bench to approved limited field test with decision points.

## Safeguards and Review

The responsible engineer approves the tests' relevance and continuation; good simulation results do not automatically give permission for production or certification.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to actionable evidence, along with transferability, test cost, and errors first discovered in the field.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
