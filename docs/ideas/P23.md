# P23 Find risk areas that the tests do not illuminate

<a id="p23"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Technology data and operation](../stages/problem.md#p-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Legacy Systems, Embedded and OT, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compare requirements, change patterns and existing test coverage to identify situations where the team lacks verification evidence. It can describe the problem as a lack of security in certain stages, so that later test work is governed by risk rather than coverage percentage alone.

## Inputs and Evidence

Requirements, test catalog, incident findings, change history and overview of critical user processes, environments and supported system variants in the solution.

## Prerequisites

Access to requirements and test purposes, traceability to critical processes and competence to assess the strength of the tests.

## Example and Deliverable

Payment tests cover the normal flow, but no interruption during confirmation. AI creates an overview of verification gaps with affected requirements and known consequences for customers.

## Safeguards and Review

The test manager checks that the tests are actually missing or insufficient; the number of tests alone says little about error detection.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to validated verification gap, along with false gaps, critical scenario coverage, and bugs that slip through.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
