# R04 Plan the sequence between dependent deliveries

<a id="r04"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Preparation and release decision](../stages/production.md#r-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Legacy Systems, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can analyze contracts, version requirements and migration plans to propose a safe sequence between several deliveries. It can point out periods where old and new versions must coexist. This supports coordination across teams without making simultaneous changes an unreasonable requirement.

## Inputs and Evidence

Version matrix, API contracts, database changes, rollout plans, team owners, and known compatibility windows between services to change.

## Prerequisites

Affected services must have known version requirements and owners, and compatibility must be confirmed before the order is approved.

## Example and Deliverable

When switching to a new event format, AI delivers a sequence plan with consumer support first, producer change afterwards and removal of the old format at the end.

## Safeguards and Review

Integration and release managers validate the sequence and breakpoints. No team is considered ready without confirmed compatibility and agreed responsibilities.

## Measuring Impact

Coordination time per compound release, along with blocked rollouts and failures due to incompatible versions during the transition period.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
