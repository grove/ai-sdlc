# H23 Test which context measures actually help

<a id="h23"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Economy capacity and working methods](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Legacy Systems, AI in the product, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can propose an experiment that compares concrete improvements in context, such as updated contracts, examples or clear demarcations. It can keep other conditions stable and formulate measurements so that the team finds out what information reduces errors or control work.

## Inputs and Evidence

Representative task set, existing context package, known context errors and measures for correctness, time consumption and human follow-up in the execution.

## Prerequisites

Versioned context packages, known quality criteria and control of the model, tools and other simultaneous changes in the experiment.

## Example and Deliverable

Two context packages for integration work are tested with the same task type. AI designs an experiment where one includes clarified zero values ​​and clear source priority.

## Safeguards and Review

The specialist checks that the variants are valid and comparable; no variant can omit safety requirements that are already mandatory.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time for bounded context testing, together with correctness, maintenance needs, context cost and necessary human control of the result.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
