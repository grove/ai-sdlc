# L02 Retain learning from experiments that don't work

<a id="l02"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Improve hypotheses and prioritization](../stages/learning.md#l-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, AI in the product

## Opportunity

AI can catalog failed, aborted and neutral trials with context and cost. It makes it possible to find out what has already been tried, and suggests when a new attempt actually has different prerequisites rather than repeating the same mistake.

## Inputs and Evidence

Experiment register, interruption reasons, learning records, task types and versions of tools and context.

## Prerequisites

Safe reporting of negative results and a searchable register with concrete assumptions.

## Example and Deliverable

Three teams have tried the same agent pattern. AI creates an experience record that shows why each attempt stopped, and what a new attempt must clarify.

## Safeguards and Review

The teams validate descriptions without placing blame. A negative result in one context does not become a general ban.

## Measuring Impact

Recorded repetitions of known blind spots per relevant trial, with quality of the context description and legitimate retrial.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
