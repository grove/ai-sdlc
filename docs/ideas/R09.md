# R09 Support controlled gradual rollout

<a id="r09"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Deployment and activation](../stages/production.md#r-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Bounded Execution
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can translate an approved rollout plan into concrete steps and explain the status between the steps. Within an explicit mandate, an agent can start pre-approved pipeline jobs and stop on deviations. This can reduce routine work while keeping critical decisions in controlled and traceable mechanisms.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved release plan, pipeline interface, user groups, control criteria, stop rules, access restrictions and confirmed operational readiness upon implementation.

## Prerequisites

A deterministic deployment pipeline, delimited service identities and explicit authorization for each relevant action area must be established.

## Example and Deliverable

An agent initiates deployment to an approved test segment and collects results from defined ports in an execution log before the next step can be authorized.

## Safeguards and Review

Limits and stops are technically enforced. Expansion of exposure requires agreed approval, and AI cannot override red gates or change the criteria.

## Measuring Impact

Manual labor time per approved rollout, along with incorrect exposure, translated stop criteria and deviations from the approved plan.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
