# K30 Shorten context without losing important requirements

<a id="k30"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Maintenance and reuse of context](../stages/context.md#k-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Legacy Systems, AI in the product

## Opportunity

AI can propose a compact representation of large documents and code areas with preservation of critical constraints and source pointers. It can make agent runs less expensive and more transparent, but only if the necessary details can still be retrieved when needed.

## Inputs and Evidence

Large source package, the task's objectives, prioritized requirements, context budget and access to the original material.

## Prerequisites

Ability to measure task quality and retrieve authoritative details without exceeding access limits.

## Example and Deliverable

For a comprehensive specification, AI creates a summary with mandatory rules, exceptions and references, while large detailed tables are only retrieved when the task requires them.

## Safeguards and Review

Critical requirements must be checked against the original; summary should not hide exceptions or turn uncertain information into certain rules.

## Measuring Impact

Context cost and retrieval time, balanced with solution quality, omitted requirements and additional clarifications.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
