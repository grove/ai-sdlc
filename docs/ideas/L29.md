# L29 Plan the change of supplier and the preservation of knowledge

<a id="l29"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Scale and manage good working practices](../stages/learning.md#l-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, AI in the product, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can use the lessons learned from a solution to document dependencies, test requirements and reusable artifacts when changing models or tools. It identifies what can be moved, what needs to be retried and how the team avoids losing its learning history.

## Inputs and Evidence

Contracts, interfaces, evaluations, data flow, tool configuration and export capabilities.

## Prerequisites

Access to approved agreement information and export of own artefacts and measurement data.

## Example and Deliverable

The team will switch agent platforms. AI creates a migration package with context format, evaluation tasks, permissions and critical compatibility tests.

## Safeguards and Review

The procurement, security and technical owner controls the current conditions. Do not assume that data, rights or model behavior is portable.

## Measuring Impact

Time and cost to verified supplier change, with data retention, quality and remaining bindings.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
