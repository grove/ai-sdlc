# H19 Check if the bottleneck just moves

<a id="h19"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Economy capacity and working methods](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can help describe a simple queue or capacity model for the workflow and propose scenarios for increased production. It can use a controlled calculation tool to investigate whether faster development is likely to increase waiting time in review, testing or approval.

## Inputs and Evidence

Measured arrivals, processing times, waiting times, variation, capacity and rules for prioritization in each significant step of the flow.

## Prerequisites

Representative flow data, known capacity limitation and competence to assess whether the chosen model is suitable.

## Example and Deliverable

The agent may deliver more PRs than the team has time to review. AI prepares a scenario model with documented assumptions and tests for review capacity.

## Safeguards and Review

Team and analyst validate the model against history; a simulation is conditioned by assumptions and is not a forecast guarantee.

## Measuring Impact

Measure modeling time and decision benefit, together with actual queue development, delivery quality and deviation from assumed capacity during the trial.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
