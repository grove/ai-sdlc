# K15 Divide work into clear agent assignments

<a id="k15"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Requirements and task understanding](../stages/context.md#k-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Legacy Systems, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can propose a division of a larger task into defined deliverables with ownership, dependencies and completion criteria. It gives the execution a manageable mandate and makes it possible to check progress without losing coherence in the end result.

## Inputs and Evidence

Overall objectives, affected components, acceptance criteria, resource frameworks and permitted tools.

## Prerequisites

A sufficiently clarified main goal and owners who can prioritize dependencies and approve the mandate.

## Example and Deliverable

A modernization is divided into contract mapping, characterization tests and small code changes; AI supplies task cards with interfaces and explicit non-targets for each part.

## Safeguards and Review

The technical manager approves the division and integration points; division should not hide risks or create pointless coordination.

## Measuring Impact

Time to approved task and blocks during execution, balanced with integration errors and coordination work.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
