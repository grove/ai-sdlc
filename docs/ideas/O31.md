# O31 Assess the degree of automation against actual additional work

<a id="o31"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Credible impact analysis](../stages/observation.md#o-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Platform Engineering, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can describe how often a task is completed within the mandate, when people intervene and why. The analysis separates meaningful points of approval from agent failures and discovers whether higher autonomy merely shifts work from execution to monitoring and cleanup.

## Inputs and Evidence

Agent incidents, human approvals, takeovers, escalations, work trials and costs.

## Prerequisites

Registration of actual takeovers and a stable definition of correct completion.

## Example and Deliverable

An agent gets more tools. AI makes a comparison of completion, control time, interruptions and outcomes before and after the access change.

## Safeguards and Review

The responsible owner assesses the necessary checks. The goal should not be the fewest possible number of people in the loop, regardless of the risk.

## Measuring Impact

Total human time per correctly completed task, with policy deviation, control quality and agent cost.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
