# R18 Prepare measurement before activating the function

<a id="r18"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Operational readiness and reception](../stages/production.md#r-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Data and Analytics, AI in the product

## Opportunity

AI can check that the observation plan can actually be carried out when the release is put into use. It connects hypotheses, events and release identity to planned measurements and alerts. This prevents a delivery from being technically available, but lacking the signals that are later needed to assess effect and damage.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hypothesis card, measurement definitions, telemetry plan, release identity, incident form, notification thresholds and requirements for data minimization and storage of measurement data.

## Prerequisites

Primary metric and balancing goals must be defined, with instrumentation that can be tested before the real user group is exposed.

## Example and Deliverable

Before activating a new search function, AI creates a checklist for the search event, result display and completed task, including release version and approved measurement of errors.

## Safeguards and Review

Analysis and service owner verifies the events with actual samples. Privacy requirements also apply to measurement data, and the lack of instrumentation must not be hidden by assumptions.

## Measuring Impact

Time to ready-to-measure delivery, together with missing events, invalid release links and proportion of hypotheses that later lack analyzable data.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
