# O16 Check whether released capacity is being used

<a id="o16"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Delivery flow and resource use](../stages/observation.md#o-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Maintenance, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can follow the agreed capacity gain until concrete application, such as more accepted deliveries, less overtime or reduced hire. It separates calculated time savings from documented benefit and makes uncertainty and overlap between profit items visible.

## Inputs and Evidence

Baseline for work effort, capacity measures, delivery data, financial decisions and registered extra work.

## Prerequisites

A named profit owner and agreement on how the released capacity is to be used.

## Example and Deliverable

The team estimates released hours. AI creates a profit track that shows which priority tasks the capacity funded, and which are just expectations.

## Safeguards and Review

Gevinsteier confirms application. The same hours cannot be valued both as cost cuts and as multiple deliveries.

## Measuring Impact

Documented realized benefit from released capacity, with total cost and outstanding work made visible.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
