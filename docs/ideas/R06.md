# R06 Choose the rollout form based on the characteristics of the change

<a id="r06"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Preparation and release decision](../stages/production.md#r-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can propose full, gradual or parallel rollout based on reversibility, user groups, dependencies and failure consequences. It makes the assumptions explicit and compares practical trade-offs. This helps the team to choose a delivery scheme that suits the change, rather than using the same method for all types of release.

## Inputs and Evidence

Risk class, scope of impact, available deployment mechanisms, compatibility requirements, observation signals and technical options for rollback or shutdown.

## Prerequisites

The team must know the reversibility of the change and have verified which deployment and stopping mechanisms the target environment actually supports.

## Example and Deliverable

For a new search component, AI creates a decision note that compares parallel traffic, small user group and direct switching, with testable conditions for each alternative.

## Safeguards and Review

Service owner approves the strategy and criteria. AI should not choose a method that requires infrastructure or rollback capabilities that the team does not actually have.

## Measuring Impact

Time to approved rollout plan, together with scope of consequences in the event of deviations, interrupted releases and number of unforeseen transition states.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
