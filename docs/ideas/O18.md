# O18 Sort noise in alarms and logs

<a id="o18"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Operational quality and risk](../stages/observation.md#o-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can group similar alarms and log patterns, suggest which signals belong to the same event and formulate a brief event picture. This can reduce manual sorting work, while retaining the original signals for checking and auditing.

## Inputs and Evidence

Alarm history, log selection, service map, severity rules and previously confirmed events.

## Prerequisites

Sufficient alarm context and possibility to return to raw signals.

## Example and Deliverable

Hundreds of alerts follow an integration failure. AI creates a grouped overview that separates clusters of symptoms from possible primary events.

## Safeguards and Review

The duty team controls grouping and priority. AI must not hide critical alerts or change alarm rules without an approved process.

## Measuring Impact

Time spent on alarm triage, with translated critical events and false groupings as control measures.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
