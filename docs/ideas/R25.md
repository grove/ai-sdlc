# R25 Explain errors encountered during deployment

<a id="r25"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Controlled error handling and termination](../stages/production.md#r-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Maintenance, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can compare deployment logs, environmental changes and rollout plans when a delivery stops or causes technical errors. It suggests likely causes and what controls will distinguish them. This reduces the briefing time in the implementation, without allowing plausible explanations to replace verified diagnosis or approved measures.

## Inputs and Evidence

Deploy logs, last approved status, configuration diff, artifact identity, environmental events and documented stops and escalation rules for the deployment.

## Prerequisites

The rollout must have traceable logs and explicit stop rules, and operators must have access to approved diagnostic checks.

## Example and Deliverable

A stopped rollout gets a diagnostic report that separates missing access from unavailable dependencies, with readable checks and links to relevant error messages.

## Safeguards and Review

The release operator confirms the diagnosis. AI shall not attempt increasingly extensive repairs, change access or repeat a risky operation without approval.

## Measuring Impact

Time from rollout failure to confirmed cause, along with incorrect actions, unnecessary rework and additional damage during handling.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
