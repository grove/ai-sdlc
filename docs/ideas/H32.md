# H32 Determine decision rules before the result arrives

<a id="h32"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Risk and decision rules](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can collect hypothesis, measurement plan, costs and risks in an experimental protocol with criteria for scaling, adjusting, continuing or stopping. It can highlight what happens when data is missing, so that the decision is not adapted afterwards to a desired result.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved hypothesis, measurement definitions, quality limits, resource framework, data needs and which persons have the authority to decide on further use.

## Prerequisites

Completed trial design, named decision-makers and a fixed follow-up date with access to measurement results and risk overview.

## Example and Deliverable

The team agrees that a pilot will be measured further in the event of great uncertainty and stopped in the event of a critical data breach. AI delivers a dated protocol with those responsible.

## Safeguards and Review

Hypothesis and risk owner approves the rules; later changes must be justified and versioned before they are used in the interpretation of findings.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to approved protocol, along with compliance, unfounded criteria changes and decisions made on insufficient data.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
