# A32 Create technical models and decision-making artifacts

<a id="a32"></a>

- **Stage:** [Agent Execution](../stages/agent-execution.md)
- **Category:** [Platform and other deliveries](../stages/agent-execution.md#a-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Regulated Systems, Data and Analytics

## Opportunity

AI can transform approved professional and technical choices into readable models, diagrams and structured documents. It can keep names and relationships consistent across artefacts, so that even projects without code delivery get concrete execution support.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved decisions, conceptual model, source references, document template and expected recipient and acceptance format.

## Prerequisites

A clarified decision-making basis and a recipient who can confirm that the artefact covers the information need.

## Example and Deliverable

An architecture work gets a computer model, a sequence diagram and a decision note with the same terms; the delivery marks open questions and which relationships have been confirmed.

## Safeguards and Review

Professional owner approves the content; diagrams and texts are suggestions until relationships, justifications and sources have been checked.

## Measuring Impact

Time to approved academic artefact, balanced with factual errors, misunderstandings by recipients and later rework.

## Applying This Idea

Define the deliverable, tools, file areas, stop signals and responsibility for review. Include setup, human follow-up and later error correction when the experiment is evaluated.
