# R32 Provide a verifiable package for recovery

<a id="r32"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Controlled error handling and termination](../stages/production.md#r-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Maintenance, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can collect approved artefacts, infrastructure references and recovery steps so that the solution can be re-established in the event of environmental loss. It can identify unresolved manual dependencies. This makes restoreability a concrete part of the production delivery, beyond the fact that the last ordinary rollout happened to be successful.

## Inputs and Evidence

Artifact registry, infrastructure version, backup routines, configuration references, recovery targets, and results from controlled exercises of service reestablishment.

## Prerequisites

The team must have access to approved artefacts and testable recovery, with defined goals and authorized people responsible for preparedness.

## Example and Deliverable

A platform delivery receives an environmental loss recovery package, with actual samples of backups, resource ordering and data reconciliation; AI shows which steps have been practiced.

## Safeguards and Review

The operations manager approves and practices the package. Secrets are safely retrieved when driving, and AI must not present unproven steps as verified readiness.

## Measuring Impact

Time to practiced reestablishment, along with outstanding manual dependencies, restored data integrity and deviations from agreed service availability targets.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
