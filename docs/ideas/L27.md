# L27 Maintain control rules from real deviations

<a id="l27"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Scale and manage good working practices](../stages/learning.md#l-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Regulated Systems, AI in the product, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can translate confirmed deviations into proposals for more precise controls, such as a blocked tool operation, new test or clearer data class. It explains what problem the control addresses and assesses possible friction before attempting the change.

## Inputs and Evidence

Safety and quality deviations, existing policy, risk acceptance, control results and work measurements.

## Prerequisites

A confirmed deviation and the opportunity to test both the desired effect and accidental blocking.

## Example and Deliverable

A document contained instruction injection. AI proposes a new test and clearer treatment of untrusted sources, with traceable justification.

## Safeguards and Review

The safety or quality manager approves the rule. AI cannot change its own policy or remove controls at its own discretion.

## Measuring Impact

Repetition of the relevant deviation type and the control's detection, with false rejections and extra work.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
