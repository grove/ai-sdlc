# H17 Model net time gain and total cost

<a id="h17"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Economy capacity and working methods](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Documents and Decisions, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can design a calculation that includes saved work tasks, new control tasks, licenses, model use and ongoing maintenance. It can separate released capacity from actual cost cuts, so that the hypothesis of efficiency gains is examined for the entire workflow and not just for the production of drafts.

## Inputs and Evidence

Baseline for working hours, expected task volume, current prices, training needs and estimates for review, error correction and agent operation in the experiment.

## Prerequisites

Clarified calculation rates, documented baseline and follow-up of all significant costs throughout the trial period.

## Example and Deliverable

A document agent makes drafts quickly, but requires professional control. AI supplies a calculation template that includes both activities and shows when the experiment yields a net benefit.

## Safeguards and Review

The finance and subject manager checks assumptions; estimated hours are capacity value and should not be booked as cash gain without realisation.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to complete cost model, together with missing records, double counting and deviations from actual resource use.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
