# P04 Uncover accessibility issues

<a id="p04"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [User needs and business problems](../stages/problem.md#p-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Mobile and Client Software, Documents and Decisions, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can structure reported barriers from user tests, accessibility reviews and support. It can connect the barriers to specific user tasks and prioritization needs, so that accessibility is understood as problems users encounter, before technical fixes are planned and verified.

## Inputs and Evidence

Accessibility findings, user tasks, test notes, supported aids and descriptions of applicable interfaces or documents.

## Prerequisites

Access to representative test findings, expertise in universal design and clarified target group for the solution.

## Example and Deliverable

Reports of keyboard usage are aggregated to the "order cannot be completed without mouse" issue. The deliverable describes the steps involved, sources and the need for further investigations.

## Safeguards and Review

The accessibility specialist confirms the consistency with actual aids and relevant users; AI does not replace such testing.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time from barrier report to priority issue, along with confirmed and translated critical barriers.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
