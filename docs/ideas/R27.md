# R27 Schedule correction when reversal is not possible

<a id="r27"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Controlled error handling and termination](../stages/production.md#r-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Data and Analytics, Legacy Systems, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can help professionals compare forward-looking remediation, data recovery and compensatory actions after irreversible changes. It highlights dependencies and possible consequences. This supports a controlled choice when a simple rollback does not restore the correct state or will result in further data loss.

## Inputs and Evidence

Confirmed failure condition, migration history, data owner rules, backups, irreversible side effects and approved options for repair or compensation.

## Prerequisites

The extent of damage must be documented, and responsible owners must have access to verifiable data and approved recovery options.

## Example and Deliverable

After incorrect mapping in a migration, AI creates a draft decision that compares corrective update with recovery, including reconciliation of changes made after the switch.

## Safeguards and Review

The data and service owner decides on the solution. Proposals are tested on secure copies, and AI shall never carry out irreversible repairs on its own initiative.

## Measuring Impact

Time to approved repair plan, along with correctly reconciled end result, valid changes lost and new errors introduced by the repair.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
