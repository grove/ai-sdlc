# L32 Develop the opportunity catalog itself

<a id="l32"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Scale and manage good working practices](../stages/learning.md#l-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Platform Engineering, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can propose new catalog ideas, merge real duplicates and update assumptions from the projects' experiences. Each idea can be given a local status such as unproven, trialled, documented useful or discouraged in a particular context, so that the catalog remains useful over time.

## Inputs and Evidence

Local experiment results, new issues, user input, updated control requirements and the catalog's version history.

## Prerequisites

A catalog ownership and a fixed form that separates idea, local testing and documented effect.

## Example and Deliverable

Several teams find a new method to improve test data. AI proposes a catalog entry with an example, source basis, control, goals and links to trials.

## Safeguards and Review

Catalog owner approves changes and preserves history. Local experiences are marked with context and do not automatically become general recommendations.

## Measuring Impact

Time for a relevant idea and proportion of reuse with documented usefulness, with duplicates and outdated advice.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
