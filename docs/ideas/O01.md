# O01 Monitor actual use of new functionality

<a id="o01"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Users and business outcomes](../stages/observation.md#o-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Mobile and Client Software, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can compile product data and qualitative feedback to describe who uses a delivered function, and who drops out. The analysis separates exposure, first attempt and repeated use, so that the team discovers the difference between publication and real use.

## Inputs and Evidence

Release information, feature flag exposure, event definitions, aggregated usage figures and approved user segments.

## Prerequisites

Stable event model and documented link between version, exposure and target group.

## Example and Deliverable

A self-service function has been launched. AI creates an adoption report that shows exposed users, completed initial tasks and repeated use with source references.

## Safeguards and Review

Product owner validates event semantics and selection. Avoid personal profiling and do not interpret a lack of tracking as a lack of use.

## Measuring Impact

Proportion of exposed users who carry out the relevant task, together with error rate and missing measurement data.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
