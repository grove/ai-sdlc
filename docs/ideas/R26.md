# R26 Prepare and support rollback to known version

<a id="r26"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Controlled error handling and termination](../stages/production.md#r-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can flesh out a rollback plan with the right artifacts, sequencing and compatibility checks. During an approved intervention, it can explain the status and collect proof of execution. This reduces the risk that a supposedly simple rollback overlooks data changes or dependencies that make rollback more complicated.

## Inputs and Evidence

Previously approved artifact, changed data and contracts, rollback criteria, dependency order, and results from exercising the rollback procedure.

## Prerequisites

A known good version and tested rollback mechanism must be found, with clear limits on which data changes can be reversed.

## Example and Deliverable

For a service release, AI creates a rollback package with the correct image identity, flag status and checking that the previous version can still read the updated form.

## Safeguards and Review

Service owner or standby role authorizes the intervention. Deterministic mechanisms perform the rollback, and irreversible data changes must be considered separately before the plan is used.

## Measuring Impact

Time to controlled rollback service, along with data loss, incorrect version and failed rollbacks due to unverified assumptions.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
