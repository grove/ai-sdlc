# O27 Analyze comparable control procedures

<a id="o27"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Credible impact analysis](../stages/observation.md#o-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Data and Analytics, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can prepare data bases and explanations for a preselected experimental design, such as randomization or stepwise rollout. It points to differences in task mix, staffing and concurrent events, while statistical calculations are made with controllable analysis tools.

## Inputs and Evidence

Frozen analysis plan, control and action data, task types, sample size and documented concurrent changes.

## Prerequisites

A credible comparison design and enough relevant observations for the desired precision.

## Example and Deliverable

Two teams introduce test agents at different times. AI delivers an analysis draft with effect intervals, the groups' previous trends and alternative explanations.

## Safeguards and Review

A person with analytical skills assesses assumptions and uncertainty. Do not claim cause when the data base only supports covariation.

## Measuring Impact

Effect on predefined primary measure with uncertainty interval, together with agreed quality limits and sample size.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
