# V24 Verify configured business processes

<a id="v24"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Specialized deliveries](../stages/verification.md#v-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Packaged Software, Integrations, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compare configuration changes in packaged software with roles, rules and end-to-end processes. It can propose acceptance tests that include the interfaces with other systems. This helps the team detect process violations that are not apparent from the vendor's standard tests for individual functions alone.

## Inputs and Evidence

Configuration diff, process map, role model, approved business rules, vendor change notes and integrations affected by the upgrade.

## Prerequisites

Configuration and local adaptations must be available, with process owners who can describe and approve real workflows.

## Example and Deliverable

After changing the approval flow in a purchasing system, AI creates test procedures for substitutes, amount limits and rejected invoices, with expected traces to the financial system.

## Safeguards and Review

Process owner approves the results in a representative environment. AI shall not assume that standard settings or supplier tests cover local adaptations.

## Measuring Impact

Time to accepted configuration change, along with business process breaks and manual workarounds that become necessary after deployment.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
