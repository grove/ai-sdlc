# R10 Control the activation and lifetime of function flags

<a id="r10"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Deployment and activation](../stages/production.md#r-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Mobile and Client Software, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can create an activation plan that links feature flags to user groups, dependencies and expiration dates. It can point out conflicting flag combinations and forgotten temporary settings. This supports the controlled availability of functionality after deploy and makes subsequent clean-up a planned part of the delivery.

## Inputs and Evidence

Flag register, target groups, contracts, permitted combinations, owner, activation criteria and planned time for removal of temporary flags.

## Prerequisites

The flags must have known owners, controllable audience management and verified mechanisms for enabling and disabling functionality.

## Example and Deliverable

For a new payment flow, AI creates a flag plan with internal pilot, approved customer segment, dependent client version and task to remove the flag after stabilization.

## Safeguards and Review

The product owner and service owner approve the exposure. Person-based segmentation must follow agreed rules, and activation only takes place through authorized control surfaces.

## Measuring Impact

Time from deploy to agreed availability, along with incorrect audience exposure, flag conflicts and number of expired flags still active.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
