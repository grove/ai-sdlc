# V11 Consider dependencies and the origin of the delivery

<a id="v11"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Safety and robustness](../stages/verification.md#v-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Legacy Systems, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compare dependency changes, scanner findings and information on package origin to prioritize control needs. It can explain which findings affect the specific use. This supports review of generated dependencies without replacing signature checks, package registries or professional assessment of terms of use.

## Inputs and Evidence

Lock files, software inventory, signatures, package sources, scan results, usage patterns, and your organization's approved rules for dependencies.

## Prerequisites

The team must have up-to-date scanners, controlled package sources, and an established process for approving new dependencies.

## Example and Deliverable

When an agent proposes a new package, AI creates a review list of unknown origin, relevant scanner findings, and the already approved option.

## Safeguards and Review

Developer and relevant subject manager verify source, findings and conditions against authoritative data. AI shall never invent packages or approvals.

## Measuring Impact

Time to clarify dependency findings, together with the proportion of unverified components and confirmed risk findings that are later discovered in the delivery.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
