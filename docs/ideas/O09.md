# O09 Follow the entire lead time from need to value

<a id="o09"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Delivery flow and resource use](../stages/observation.md#o-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can collect events from need, task, code review, build and actual use into a timeline. It makes differences between active work and queue visible, so that the team can see whether AI shortens the entire process or just one of the sub-steps.

## Inputs and Evidence

Case history, PR and release identifiers, acceptance times, exposure data and stable status definitions.

## Prerequisites

A common change identifier and reliable timestamps throughout the relevant flow.

## Example and Deliverable

Implementation is faster, but waiting for acceptance has increased. AI creates a flowchart with time distribution and links to underlying events.

## Safeguards and Review

The team verifies links and status semantics. Given time stamps must not be interpreted as recorded working hours.

## Measuring Impact

Median and p85 time to accepted value, with delivered quality and task mix as control measures.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
