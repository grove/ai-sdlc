# P09 Find bottlenecks in the entire delivery flow

<a id="p09"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Bottlenecks and workflow](../stages/problem.md#p-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Maintenance, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compile status transitions and describe where work accumulates or waits. It can highlight how different types of work move from need to use, so that the team examines the constraint that affects overall flow the most.

## Inputs and Evidence

Status history, definitions of work steps, delivery times, work types and recorded periods with errors or missing time data.

## Prerequisites

Stable work definitions, coherent history and the possibility to validate patterns with those who carry out the work.

## Example and Deliverable

Assignments are quickly coded, but wait a long time for acceptance. AI creates a flow note with waiting distribution, affected task types and data limitations for the team.

## Safeguards and Review

The team checks that the status actually means waiting or working; calendar days must not be mistakenly interpreted as active working time.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to confirmed bottleneck, along with end-to-end flow time and data quality in the status recording.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
