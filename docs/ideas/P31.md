# P31 Compare problem candidates with visible basis

<a id="p31"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Costs, risk and prioritization](../stages/problem.md#p-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can structure candidate problems according to affected users, documented consequence, expected effort and uncertainty. It can show exchange rates and missing data, so that the prioritization becomes easier to discuss without hiding difficult value choices in one aggregate score.

## Inputs and Evidence

Validated problem cards, product targets, capacity, risk, cost intervals and known commitments in the portfolio or delivery plan for the period.

## Prerequisites

Comparable problem cards, explicit priority criteria and a decision owner who can assess value and risk.

## Example and Deliverable

The team considers three problems with different user importance and uncertainty. AI creates a comparison table with sources, dependencies and questions that the decision-makers must decide on.

## Safeguards and Review

The product owner determines the prioritization and weighting; the model should not present discretionary estimates as precise or objective truths.

## Measuring Impact

Measure decision-making time and clarity in the prioritization basis, together with translated consequences and later reprioritization of the same problems.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
