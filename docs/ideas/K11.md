# K11 Make quality requirements concrete for the task

<a id="k11"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Requirements and task understanding](../stages/context.md#k-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Mobile and Client Software, Embedded and OT

## Opportunity

AI can find which performance, robustness, availability, maintainability and compatibility requirements are affected by a task. It can suggest how the requirements are expressed observably and which measurement conditions must be clarified before the agent begins work.

## Inputs and Evidence

Overall quality requirements, architecture, operational targets, load profile and affected user tasks.

## Prerequisites

Existing quality responsibility and access to representative operating conditions or clearly marked estimates.

## Example and Deliverable

For an export function, AI creates a requirements draft for data volume, interruptions, resource consumption and resumption, with fields for approved limits and test environment.

## Safeguards and Review

The technical and product owner sets thresholds; proposed limits are not applicable requirements until they are decided.

## Measuring Impact

Time to complete quality criteria, along with errors due to translated requirements or unrealistic thresholds.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
