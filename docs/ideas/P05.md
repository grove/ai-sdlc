# P05 Map manual duplication of work in business processes

<a id="p05"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [User needs and business problems](../stages/problem.md#p-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Packaged Software, Integrations, Product Development, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can read process descriptions and interviews to identify registration of the same information multiple times, manual checks and copying between systems. It can describe which user tasks create work, and which exceptions prevent simple automation.

## Inputs and Evidence

Process maps, representative work descriptions, time trials, forms and an overview of the systems used in the process.

## Prerequisites

Representative work mapping, access to the process owner and clear rules for processing business data.

## Example and Deliverable

An order is registered in the CRM, financial system and a spreadsheet. AI prepares an overview of double registration, responsibility, time spent and documented exceptions in the process.

## Safeguards and Review

Process Owner verifies actual workflow and required control actions; current mandatory controls shall not be misclassified as waste.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to validated process map and identified duplicate work, together with translated statutory or professional controls.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
