# O07 Look for consequences in other work processes

<a id="o07"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Users and business outcomes](../stages/observation.md#o-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Packaged Software, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can compare local gains with changes in subsequent processes and neighboring teams. The analysis examines whether faster deliveries create manual reconciliation, more handovers or higher costs elsewhere, and makes such shifts visible to the Value stream owner.

## Inputs and Evidence

Process maps, aggregated work logs, integration errors, support volume and measurements from affected neighboring teams.

## Prerequisites

Agreed process limits and collaboration on measurements across teams.

## Example and Deliverable

Automated order creation is faster. AI finds several manual corrections in the invoicing and creates a report on the overall workload.

## Safeguards and Review

Affected process owners validate the connection. Simultaneous changes and different case volumes must be assessed before the effect is attributed to the measure.

## Measuring Impact

Total working time per correct process outcome, with errors and waiting time with the receiving team.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
