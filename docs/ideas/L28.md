# L28 Improve portfolio investment decisions

<a id="l28"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Scale and manage good working practices](../stages/learning.md#l-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compile documented effect, uncertainty, investment and maintenance across measures. It shows which improvements can be continued, which need more evidence and which should be terminated, with a clear distinction between capacity, product value and cash flow.

## Inputs and Evidence

Experiment results, approved costs, profit follow-up, risk and the portfolio's strategic goals.

## Prerequisites

Uniform terms for costs and benefits as well as honest reporting of negative trials.

## Example and Deliverable

The organization has several AI pilots. AI creates a decision-making basis that shows total cost, realized benefit, open assumptions and dependencies.

## Safeguards and Review

The portfolio and finance manager decide. Do not add overlapping gains or compare disparate units as if they were the same.

## Measuring Impact

Documented benefits per relevant investment, with risk, uncertainty and lasting operating costs as control measures.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
