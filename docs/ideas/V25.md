# V25 Sort test failures by probable cause

<a id="v25"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Verification process and evidence](../stages/verification.md#v-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Maintenance, Legacy Systems

## Opportunity

AI can group failed test runs based on logs, change diffs and historical patterns. It suggests which findings are due to product defects, environmental problems or unstable tests, with visible justifications. This can reduce repetitive troubleshooting and help the team fix the right problem first.

## Inputs and Evidence

Test logs, error traces, change history, environmental information, previous runs and known unstable tests with documented causes.

## Prerequisites

Logs and test history must be correlative, and the team must be able to reproduce errors with known code and environment.

## Example and Deliverable

After a library upgrade, AI groups fifty red tests into three failure patterns and delivers a triage report with reproduction steps for each pattern.

## Safeguards and Review

Developer confirms the cause through reproduction. No tests are removed, ignored or marked as unstable just because AI suggests it.

## Measuring Impact

Time from red test to confirmed cause, along with misclassification and product failures that are wrongly dismissed as test instability.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
