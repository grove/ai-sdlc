# K10 Detect conflicting and ambiguous requirements

<a id="k10"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Requirements and task understanding](../stages/context.md#k-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Regulated Systems, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can compare requirement texts, contracts and examples to find contradictions, unclear concepts and missing priorities. It can deliver targeted clarification questions that reduce the likelihood that the agent will choose a random interpretation during development.

## Inputs and Evidence

Requirements documents, user stories, subject rules, illustrations and priority between relevant sources.

## Prerequisites

Access to the competing sources and an agreed channel for quick professional clarifications.

## Example and Deliverable

An order flow requires both immediate deletion and later recovery; AI creates a conflict list with sources and possible clarifications for the responsible professional.

## Safeguards and Review

The right decision owner resolves conflicts; similar wording is not automatically treated as having the same meaning in different parts of the domain.

## Measuring Impact

Number of requirement conflicts discovered before start-up and subsequent rework, balanced by the proportion of irrelevant findings.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
