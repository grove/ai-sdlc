# R30 Close temporary rights and resources after delivery

<a id="r30"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Controlled error handling and termination](../stages/production.md#r-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can compare the release's plan with temporary environments, accounts and exceptions that were created during the implementation. It creates a shutdown list with owners and confirmations. This reduces the risk of emergency accesses, test resources and temporary data being left when the delivery has been put into use.

## Inputs and Evidence

Change log, time-limited access, resource register, exception approvals, storage rules and documented need to retain data or environments.

## Prerequisites

Temporary resources and access must be marked upon creation, with owners, expiry date and a clarified process for safe termination.

## Example and Deliverable

After a migration, AI creates a closing list with revocation of additional write access, deletion of approved temporary copies and confirmation from each resource owner.

## Safeguards and Review

Owners approve cleanup prior to deletion or revocation. Conservation requirements and necessary tracks must be clarified, and AI must not arbitrarily clear production.

## Measuring Impact

Time to closed release cleanup, along with expired accesses, residual unnecessary cost, and accidental deletion of required resources or evidence.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
