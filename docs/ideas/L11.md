# L11 Remove expired advice from the knowledge base

<a id="l11"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Maintain knowledge and competence](../stages/learning.md#l-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can look for recipes, documents and instructions that are contradicted by recent decisions or have not been validated for a long time. It creates a cleanup queue of suggested replacements so that old recommendations don't continue to affect both people and agents.

## Inputs and Evidence

Knowledge register, versions, validity dates, usage tracks and approved change decisions.

## Prerequisites

A register that can distinguish current practice from historical documentation.

## Example and Deliverable

An old authentication wizard is still used. AI creates a deviation with a link to the new standard, affected context packages and proposed de-publication.

## Safeguards and Review

Subject owner decides on deletion or archiving and preserves the necessary history. Lack of use is not the only reason for removing critical knowledge.

## Measuring Impact

Proportion of used guidance with a valid owner and assessment, with errors from outdated knowledge and clearing costs.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
