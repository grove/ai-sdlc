# R17 Prepare operating procedures for the new version

<a id="r17"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Operational readiness and reception](../stages/production.md#r-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Platform Engineering, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can adapt approved operating procedures to the changes in a release and identify new error situations that must be described. It links symptoms, controls and escalation points to the current solution. This can reduce the time operations personnel spend understanding how to handle the new version.

## Inputs and Evidence

Existing runbooks, approved change description, dependency map, service level requirements, verified commands and agreed responsibility for operation and escalation.

## Prerequisites

There must be a named operator, a testable procedure and documented limits for permitted measures in production.

## Example and Deliverable

After introducing a queue, AI creates an operating draft with control of queue depth, handling of stopped consumers and a clear limit for when the data owner is contacted.

## Safeguards and Review

The operator validates the procedure in a suitable environment. Dangerous commands and production interventions are marked with necessary permissions and should not be activated by the documentation alone.

## Measuring Impact

Time to approved operating procedure, along with missing action steps and errors that occur when the procedure is tested by the receiving team.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
