# K24 Create precise handovers between sessions

<a id="k24"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Sources of data and work surface](../stages/context.md#k-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Legacy Systems, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can summarize completed work, decisions, open questions and tool results when a developer or agent has to hand over a task. A controlled handover reduces the need to reconstruct history and makes it easier to continue without duplicate work.

## Inputs and Evidence

Work log, current diff, decisions, executed commands and remaining acceptance criteria.

## Prerequisites

A traceable work log and requirement to preserve evidence of executed commands and decisions.

## Example and Deliverable

At the end of an agent session, a handover is delivered with commit, test status, unresolved errors and the next recommended action, so that the next session starts concretely.

## Safeguards and Review

Responsible developer confirms status; proposed or planned work is clearly marked and must not be referred to as completed.

## Measuring Impact

Time for resumed productive work, along with duplicate work, wrong status and lost clarifications.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
