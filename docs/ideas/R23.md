# R23 Make knowledge deliverables ready for actual use

<a id="r23"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Operational readiness and reception](../stages/production.md#r-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Product Development, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can help with the publication and formal reception of deliverables such as architecture decisions, test strategies or research results. It makes consequences, scope and next action clear to the recipient. This provides a concrete production step even when the project's result is a usable decision or knowledge rather than running code.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved document or prototype, basis for decision, target group, area of validity, recipient, planned use and criteria for accepting the delivery.

## Prerequisites

The delivery's recipient and user interface must be defined, with a clear distinction between decision, draft, prototype and approved operational solution.

## Example and Deliverable

An architecture work ends with a published decision, implementation tasks and confirmed owner; AI prepares the reception package that shows which teams must deal with the election.

## Safeguards and Review

Subject owner confirms the decision and the recipient accepts the use. Publication alone does not prove usefulness, and a prototype must not appear as a production-approved solution.

## Measuring Impact

Time to confirmed receipt and first planned use, along with unclear decisions and subsequent rework due to defective handover.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
