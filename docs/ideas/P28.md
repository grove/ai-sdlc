# P28 Make uncertainty that hinders project decisions visible

<a id="p28"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Costs, risk and prioritization](../stages/problem.md#p-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Legacy Systems, Data and Analytics, Integrations, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can read decision-making bases and project plans to distinguish documented conditions from assumptions. It can point out uncertainties about data, dependencies or feasibility that stop progress, so that the project can formulate a clear research problem rather than guesswork.

## Inputs and Evidence

Decision notes, risk log, plans, open questions and documented answers from professionals, suppliers or affected service owners.

## Prerequisites

Access to the current decision-making basis, source versions and people who can confirm which information is missing.

## Example and Deliverable

A migration is at a standstill because data volume and downtime requirements are unknown. AI creates an uncertainty map with source basis, owners and specific missing clarifications.

## Safeguards and Review

The project manager and subject managers confirm what is actually unclear; absence in the documents does not necessarily mean a lack of knowledge.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to clear research problem, together with pending decisions and misclassified assumptions in the foundation.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
