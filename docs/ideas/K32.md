# K32 Adapt context standard to the project type

<a id="k32"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Maintenance and reuse of context](../stages/context.md#k-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Embedded and OT, Mobile and Client Software, Documents and Decisions, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can propose which common context requirements apply to a project, and which additions follow from the scope of delivery and criticality. It makes reuse of standards practical without treating a mobile app, firmware and a decision memo as equal tasks.

## Inputs and Evidence

Project profile, delivery surface, data classes, approved internal standards and roles for professional approval.

## Prerequisites

A maintained common standard and the possibility to approve documented local adaptations.

## Example and Deliverable

For an embedded project, AI creates a context draft with hardware version, time requirements and test bench, while a document project receives source requirements, decision owner and acceptance format.

## Safeguards and Review

The project owner and subject managers confirm the profile; AI cannot downgrade risk or exempt the project from mandatory requirements.

## Measuring Impact

Time for the correct work setup, together with missing project requirements and unnecessary documentation work.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
