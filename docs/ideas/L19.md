# L19 Choose a model based on documented mastery of tasks

<a id="l19"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Improve agents and work tools](../stages/learning.md#l-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Platform Engineering, Data and Analytics

## Opportunity

AI can compile its own evaluation results for approved models and suggest which task types each is suitable for. The team can try simpler or less expensive models where they meet requirements, while computing, response time and quality variation are explicitly considered.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved model register, task set, quality results, actual pricing, latency and data requirements.

## Prerequisites

Several approved alternatives and representative evaluations of the actual tasks.

## Example and Deliverable

An expensive model is used for simple classification. AI makes a comparison of alternatives based on local test results and cost per correct answer.

## Safeguards and Review

Platform and subject owner decide route selection. Verify current agreements and price, and test the change before moving traffic.

## Measuring Impact

Cost per correctly completed task, with error rate, data requirements and response time as hard control points.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
