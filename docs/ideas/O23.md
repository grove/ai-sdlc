# O23 Monitor quality and biases in AI products

<a id="o23"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Operational quality and risk](../stages/observation.md#o-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Data and Analytics, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can structure validated user complaints, evaluations and outcome data for a product that itself contains AI. It can highlight changes in response utility, rejections or errors between use situations and suggest which parts of the evaluation basis should be examined.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved evaluations, product versions, user feedback, outcome data and legally established relevant groups.

## Prerequisites

Representative evaluation and a process for human assessment of actual outcomes.

## Example and Deliverable

An internal search assistant loses quality on rare subject terms. AI creates a quality overview with controlled examples and categories for further evaluation.

## Safeguards and Review

Subject experts evaluate a blinded sample. The same model cannot alone determine its own quality or derive sensitive user groups.

## Measuring Impact

Validated task success per use situation, with incorrect information, unwanted rejections and relevant differential treatment.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
