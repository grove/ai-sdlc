# R29 Coordinate secure rotation of keys and certificates

<a id="r29"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Controlled error handling and termination](../stages/production.md#r-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Integrations, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can structure rotation of certificates and service accesses with overlap, dependencies and verification of consumers. It works with metadata rather than secret values. This can reduce coordination errors when a release requires new identities or when old permissions are to expire without breaking service.

## Inputs and Evidence

Access metadata, expiry dates, consumer overview, approved rotation process, overlap rules and results from tests of new identity or certificate chain.

## Prerequisites

Dependencies and owners must be known, with an established secrecy system and tested procedure for overlap and revocation.

## Example and Deliverable

When exchanging certificates, AI creates a sequence plan for the new chain, consumer control and recall of the old version, without private keys being included in the context.

## Safeguards and Review

The identity or security officer approves the plan. Secrets are handled by approved systems, and any change of access requires explicit authorization.

## Measuring Impact

Coordination time per successful rotation, along with service interruptions, forgotten old accesses, and exposure of secret values during execution.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
