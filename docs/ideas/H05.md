# H05 Prioritize assumptions that should be tested first

<a id="h05"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Alternatives and mode of operation](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Legacy Systems, Data and Analytics, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can create a register of the assumptions a solution is based on and compare uncertainty, consequence and investigation cost. It can suggest which assumptions the team should clarify first, so that costly implementation does not start while crucial matters are still unclear.

## Inputs and Evidence

Solution proposals, risk log, available evidence, dependencies and a rough estimate of cost by examining each assumption.

## Prerequisites

Explicit solution requirements, professional ownership and available research that can actually reduce the current uncertainty.

## Example and Deliverable

A planned assistant assumes that current routines exist digitally and are unambiguous. AI creates an assumption register and suggests checking the sources first.

## Safeguards and Review

Decision maker considers weighting and consequence; AI-generated scores must have an explanation and must not hide a lack of knowledge.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time for clarification of critical assumptions, together with unnecessary investigations and work started on the wrong basis.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
