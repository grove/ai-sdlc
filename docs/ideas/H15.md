# H15 Plan comparison of different task types

<a id="h15"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Experiments and measurement baseline](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Legacy Systems, Integrations, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can help the team describe task mix and identify characteristics that may affect time or quality independently of AI. It can propose few professionally meaningful segments, so that the result does not hide the fact that the measure works differently for different types of work.

## Inputs and Evidence

Task history, complexity descriptions, experience, code areas, risk classes and dependencies that may affect execution or necessary verification in the experiment.

## Prerequisites

Sufficient task descriptions, consistent classification and analytical skills to handle few observations and multiple comparisons.

## Example and Deliverable

A code agent is tested on both simple fixes and obscure integration tasks. AI creates a segmentation plan with criteria that are determined before the results are analyzed.

## Safeguards and Review

Team and analyst approve the segments; avoid post-constructed groups or individual rankings that present random variations as findings.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to relevant segmentation, together with amount of data per group, classification error and stability in the impact assessment.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
