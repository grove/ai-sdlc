# L26 Standardize patterns with a clear scope

<a id="l26"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Scale and manage good working practices](../stages/learning.md#l-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Regulated Systems, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can gather several local experiences into a proposal for a common standard, including which task types and risks it covers. The standard proposal receives evidence, exceptions, owner and revision date, so that the organization scales a documented way of working and can change it later.

## Inputs and Evidence

Validated local trials, quality results, costs, deviations and relevant control requirements.

## Prerequisites

Several relevant experiences and ownership of support, auditing and non-conformance management.

## Example and Deliverable

Several teams succeed with the same PR setup. AI makes a standard proposal with minimum requirements, different risk profiles and a test with a new team.

## Safeguards and Review

The standards manager approves after local validation. One successful pilot team is not enough to claim general effect.

## Measuring Impact

Effect and control quality when re-using, with exceptional needs and cost when maintaining the standard.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
