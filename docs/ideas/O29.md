# O29 Follow if the gain lasts over time

<a id="o29"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Credible impact analysis](../stages/observation.md#o-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Legacy Systems, Product Development, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can compare short-term results with later maintenance, new errors and repeated use. It examines whether learning costs decrease, whether quality is maintained and whether the solution creates lasting simplification, so that a promising pilot result is not automatically assumed to last.

## Inputs and Evidence

Several measurement windows, change history, maintenance work, user outcomes and versions of the working method.

## Prerequisites

Planned follow-up after pilot and traceability for later work on the same delivery.

## Example and Deliverable

A refactoring saves time in the first month. AI creates a follow-up report on new changes, understanding needs and errors in the same modules.

## Safeguards and Review

Technical owner validates changed task mix and staffing. Long-term effects must be observed, not extrapolated from one successful week.

## Measuring Impact

Ongoing cost and time per comparable change, with technical debt and manufacturing defects.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
