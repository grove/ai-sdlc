# H01 Design alternative paths to the same goal

<a id="h01"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Alternatives and mode of operation](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Packaged Software, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can propose multiple solutions for one confirmed problem, including process change, simpler interface, reuse, and AI support. It can make working methods and assumptions clear, so that the project examines alternatives before committing to a specific technology.

## Inputs and Evidence

Problem brief, desired user outcomes, existing solutions, technical limitations and available capacity to investigate alternative approaches.

## Prerequisites

Clarified problem, available professionals and freedom to consider solutions beyond the first proposed tool.

## Example and Deliverable

Long response times to internal questions are met with a better document structure, search or conversation assistant. AI creates an alternative note with assumptions and possible pilot tests for the team.

## Safeguards and Review

Product owner and subject managers confirm feasibility; AI must include the current solution as a reference and highlight uncertainty in the alternatives.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to considered alternatives, together with real options and the cost of translating simpler solutions.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
