# H30 Design experiments for AI embedded in the product

<a id="h30"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Risk and decision rules](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Product Development, Data and Analytics, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can support the design of product trials where the solution itself uses models, by separating task quality, user outcomes, response time, cost and unwanted behavior. It can suggest evaluation questions and representative situations, so that the project examines both model performance and actual user value.

## Inputs and Evidence

Product needs, permitted use, representative tasks, known failure scenarios, approved data and requirements for cost, quality and human control.

## Prerequisites

Limited product role, representative evaluation tasks and professionals who can assess results independently of the model's own assessments.

## Example and Deliverable

A case assistant must propose summaries. AI creates an experimental plan that separates factual accuracy, saved reading time, need for correction and risk of important information being omitted.

## Safeguards and Review

The product and risk manager approves the evaluations; the model that is assessed should not alone define or confirm the quality of one's own answers.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to complete product trial, along with user outcomes, serious errors, cost per task and independence of evaluation.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
