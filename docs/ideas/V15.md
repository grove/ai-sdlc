# V15 Consider accessibility in real tasks

<a id="v15"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Safety and robustness](../stages/verification.md#v-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Mobile and Client Software, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can combine automatic accessibility findings with review of texts, focus order and user tasks. It can suggest manual tests for conditions scanners cannot determine on their own. This helps the team prioritize obstacles that may actually make the solution difficult or impossible to use.

## Inputs and Evidence

Interface, accessibility requirements, scanner findings, language variants, keyboard sequence and known needs of users of assistive devices.

## Prerequisites

The team needs defined accessibility requirements, access to necessary aids and people who can verify actual user progress.

## Example and Deliverable

For an order form, AI creates a checklist that links unmarked fields and unclear error messages to tests with a keyboard and screen reader.

## Safeguards and Review

Competent people carry out manual tests and evaluate the user experience. AI or an automatic scanner alone does not document full accessibility.

## Measuring Impact

Time to identified and resolved accessibility barriers, along with task completion in relevant user tests and errors after launch.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
