# P08 Investigate features that provide little observed utility

<a id="p08"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [User needs and business problems](../stages/problem.md#p-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Legacy Systems, Packaged Software, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can compare function use, maintenance work and user needs to identify areas where the cost seems high in relation to the observed value. It may suggest issues that should be investigated, including whether infrequent use is due to seasonality, lack of discoverability or special user groups.

## Inputs and Evidence

Function use, support cases, maintenance costs, target groups, seasonal patterns and the reason why the function was originally established.

## Prerequisites

Usage measurement that separates functions, understandable cost distribution and available owners for affected business processes.

## Example and Deliverable

A report module is rarely used, but requires many upgrades. AI prepares an investigation note on use, cost and possible explanations before phasing out is considered.

## Safeguards and Review

Product owner critically checks infrequent use and contractual obligations; low use alone does not provide grounds for removing functionality.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to clarified value or problem, together with translated critical needs and the quality of the cost base.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
