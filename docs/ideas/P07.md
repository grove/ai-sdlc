# P07 Finding underrepresented user situations

<a id="p07"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [User needs and business problems](../stages/problem.md#p-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Mobile and Client Software, Embedded and OT, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compare who is mentioned in requirements and insight material with the user groups the product is supposed to support. It can highlight poorly covered situations, such as infrequent use, unstable connection or language needs, and suggest targeted further insight work.

## Inputs and Evidence

Target group description, existing user insight, requirements, usage statistics at a properly aggregated level and known operating conditions.

## Prerequisites

Explicit target group, an overview of the existing selection and the opportunity to obtain real supplementary insight.

## Example and Deliverable

A field app has only been tested with experienced users with stable coverage. AI creates a gap memo for new employees, offline use and alternative work situations.

## Safeguards and Review

Product owner assesses relevance and representativeness; AI-generated personas are treated as questions and never as actual user research.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time until identified insight gap, together with later confirmation and cost of irrelevant additional mapping.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
