# L01 End hypotheses with a clear conclusion

<a id="l01"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Improve hypotheses and prioritization](../stages/learning.md#l-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Regulated Systems, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can structure a final learning item that shows what the hypothesis assumed, which findings support it and what is still uncertain. It distinguishes the rejected hypothesis from missing data and proposes a decision that the owner can justify.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hypothesis card, observation package, decision rules, deviations and verified cost and quality measurements.

## Prerequisites

A registered hypothesis and an observational basis that shows both outcomes and limitations.

## Example and Deliverable

A test attempt gave a lower time consumption, but an uncertain error rate. AI writes a learning record that justifies further measurement rather than unconditional scaling.

## Safeguards and Review

The owner approves the conclusion and preserves negative findings. AI must not change the original goal to get a positive result.

## Measuring Impact

Time from approved analysis to decided learning and allocated measures, with verifiable justification and unfounded conclusions.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
