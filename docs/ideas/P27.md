# P27 Map problems from technological expiration and upgrades

<a id="p27"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Costs, risk and prioritization](../stages/problem.md#p-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Legacy Systems, Platform Engineering, Packaged Software, Embedded and OT

## Opportunity

AI can compare supplier notifications and local component usage to show which solutions are affected by expiring support or notified changes. It can separate confirmed deadlines from uncertain information and make the need for upgrading concrete before the project chooses measures.

## Inputs and Evidence

Current official supplier notifications, component versions, contract terms, system owners and local documentation of dependencies in operation and development.

## Prerequisites

Updated component inventory, access to primary sources, and ownership of each service that may be affected.

## Example and Deliverable

A framework loses support while multiple services use different versions. AI provides an overview of affected services, confirmed dates and necessary clarifications.

## Safeguards and Review

Technical owner controls current primary sources and own contracts; AI memory alone must not be used for deadlines or support conditions.

## Measuring Impact

Measure mapping time and proportion of confirmed dependencies, along with translated services, source errors and unnecessary upgrade work.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
