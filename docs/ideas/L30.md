# L30 Eliminate ways of working that do not provide sufficient value

<a id="l30"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Scale and manage good working practices](../stages/learning.md#l-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Maintenance, AI in the product

## Opportunity

AI can identify persistently weak measures and help the owner plan the winding down of agents, licenses or recipes. The plan preserves the necessary knowledge and control, removes access and describes how the work is handled after the measure is removed.

## Inputs and Evidence

Long-term measurements, costs, usage data, dependencies, access overview and previous decisions.

## Prerequisites

Satisfactory history and overview of who or what depends on the measure.

## Example and Deliverable

An agent is rarely used and requires a lot of support. AI creates a retirement plan with alternative workflow, data management and follow-up of any lost profits.

## Safeguards and Review

Responsible owner approves retirement and controls hidden dependencies. Deletion and access changes follow an explicit mandate.

## Measuring Impact

Reduced total cost after decommissioning, with quality, remaining needs and any additional work.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
