# H03 Formulate a testable improvement hypothesis

<a id="h03"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Alternatives and mode of operation](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Documents and Decisions, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can transform a proposed measure into an if-then-because formulation with target group, expected direction, measurement window and quality limits. It can identify vague words like faster or better and suggest how to flesh them out without making up effect sizes.

## Inputs and Evidence

Problem card, baseline or collection plan, desired measures, current types of work and requirements for quality, costs and risk.

## Prerequisites

A defined problem, available measurement baseline and a named owner who can determine the decision criteria.

## Example and Deliverable

The proposal for Using AI for requirements work becomes a testable hypothesis for a less time spent clarifying requirements for integration cases, with no increase in rework and agreed rules for comparison.

## Safeguards and Review

Hypothesis owner determines meaningful effect and limit values; AI must mark proposed figures as design choices and not expected gains.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to approved hypothesis, together with measurability, subsequent change of criteria and incorrect effect claims.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
