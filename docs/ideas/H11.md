# H11 Design a trial with random assignment

<a id="h11"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Experiments and measurement baseline](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Documents and Decisions, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can propose how tasks or team periods are distributed randomly between a new way of working and a comparison process. It can help with the allocation plan, blocking by task type and analysis prerequisites, so that the experiment has a better basis for examining effectiveness.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hypothesis, realistic task volume, types of work, risk requirements and information about learning or collaboration that may affect the groups.

## Prerequisites

Sufficient task volume, practical opportunity for comparison and expertise in experimental design and relevant statistical analysis.

## Example and Deliverable

Suitable documentation tasks are divided between regular work and AI-supported drafts. AI creates an allocation protocol with criteria for inclusion, measurement and handling of deviations from the plan.

## Safeguards and Review

The method manager approves the design and consideration of people; consider learning, collaboration, and task dependency before randomizing individual tasks.

## Measuring Impact

Measure planning time and adherence to allocation rules, along with comparability, analysis uncertainty, and burden on the team's work.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
