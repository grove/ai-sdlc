# K05 Retrieve relevant architecture decisions

<a id="k05"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [System and domain](../stages/context.md#k-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Integrations, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can find previous architecture decisions, justifications and validity limits that are relevant to a task. It can describe which choices the team must respect, which alternatives were previously discarded, and which require a new decision.

## Inputs and Evidence

Architecture decisions with status and owner, system map, technical standards and task description.

## Prerequisites

Decision sources with identifiable status, date and person responsible or a process to get this clarified.

## Example and Deliverable

When planning a new message exchange, AI delivers a decision summary that explains why existing queuing technology was chosen and which limitations still apply.

## Safeguards and Review

Architect confirms validity; older discussions are not treated as binding decisions without documented status.

## Measuring Impact

Time to relevant decision-making basis, together with the number of outdated guidelines and new solutions that violate approved architecture.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
