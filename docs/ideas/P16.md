# P16 Finding obstacles in onboarding and knowledge search

<a id="p16"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Bottlenecks and workflow](../stages/problem.md#p-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Legacy Systems, Platform Engineering, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can compile questions from new employees, start-up tasks and feedback on documentation. It can identify knowledge that is difficult to find or only exists with certain people, so that the team prioritizes concrete problems in onboarding and knowledge sharing.

## Inputs and Evidence

Onboarding plans, voluntary feedback, searches without hits, support questions and an overview of approved knowledge sources in the team.

## Prerequisites

Current onboarding history, clarified use of feedback and access to people who know the actual start-up.

## Example and Deliverable

New developers use several attempts to run the system locally. AI makes a gap note on missing clarifications and questions that are repeated.

## Safeguards and Review

New and experienced employees confirm the findings; limit personal data and avoid using learning needs as individual performance assessment.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to clarify knowledge gap, together with time to first approved task and perceived friction.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
