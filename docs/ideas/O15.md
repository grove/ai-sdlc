# O15 Calculate the total cost per approved delivery

<a id="o15"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Delivery flow and resource use](../stages/observation.md#o-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Data and Analytics

## Opportunity

AI can propose cost links and explain a calculation that includes licenses, model calls, infrastructure, training, maintenance, control and debugging. The calculation is carried out in a verifiable tool and uses comparable deliveries as denominators, so that costs are not hidden between budgets.

## Inputs and Evidence

Actual costs, agreed distribution keys, work samples, task types and number of accepted deliveries.

## Prerequisites

Access to real costs and a delivery unit with a stable definition.

## Example and Deliverable

A test initiative has a low model cost, but requires maintenance. AI creates a cost account with visible distribution assumptions and sensitivity analysis.

## Safeguards and Review

Finance and team approve distribution and prevent double counting. Do not mix investment costs and running costs without showing the period.

## Measuring Impact

Total cost per comparable accepted delivery, with error rate and scope of delivery as control measures.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
