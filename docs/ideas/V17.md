# V17 Verify data quality throughout the pipeline

<a id="v17"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Specialized deliveries](../stages/verification.md#v-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Data and Analytics, Integrations, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compare data contracts, transformations and measurement results to propose and explain quality controls. It examines, among other things, completeness, validity, duplicates and temporal consistency. This can reveal when a pipeline is technically successful but delivers data that does not support its intended use.

## Inputs and Evidence

Data contracts, source descriptions, transformation code, quality profiles, data origin and representative samples approved for AI processing.

## Prerequisites

The data sources must have known owners, explicit quality requirements and verifiable examples of expected content after transformation.

## Example and Deliverable

For a customer pipeline, AI creates a quality report that shows that valid customers disappear in a join, and suggests a check with known reference cases.

## Safeguards and Review

Data owner approves quality thresholds and exceptions. Testers must check actual data products, and the model must not receive unnecessary personal data.

## Measuring Impact

Time to localized data quality failure, along with incorrect records or lost links reaching the accepted data product.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
