# H21 Comparing different degrees of agent autonomy

<a id="h21"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Economy capacity and working methods](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Product Development, Maintenance, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can design experiments that compare consulting, local editing and limited agent execution on the same type of work. It can highlight differences in control, coordination and verification, so that the team examines which autonomy gives the best overall result for the task.

## Inputs and Evidence

Representative tasks, permitted tools, risk class, approval points and baseline for both execution and necessary human follow-up.

## Prerequisites

Clarified agent policy, comparable tasks and measurement that includes human control and correction in all variants.

## Example and Deliverable

For dependency updates, AI suggestions are compared with agent-created PR. AI creates an experimental setup that measures total completion time, control needs and quality for both methods of work.

## Safeguards and Review

The technical and safety manager sets the upper autonomy limit; the attempt does not give the agent new rights without explicit approval.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to validated trial design, along with total labor time, rework, unwanted side effects and actual net cost.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
