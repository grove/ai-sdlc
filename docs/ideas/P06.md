# P06 Separate needs from proposed solutions

<a id="p06"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [User needs and business problems](../stages/problem.md#p-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Packaged Software, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can review orders that already specify a feature or technology, and formulate the problem as the desired change for a user or business. It can point to missing documentation and questions that must be answered before the need is clarified.

## Inputs and Evidence

Orders, business goals, user descriptions, available impact data and decisions that explain why the case was created.

## Prerequisites

Access to the customer, understandable business goals and room to investigate the problem before choosing a solution.

## Example and Deliverable

The order "build a chatbot" is reformulated into problems with finding current routines. The delivery is a requirement note with proof, uncertainty and delimitation.

## Safeguards and Review

Orderer and affected users approve the reformulation; AI may not replace explicit requirements with its own assumptions.

## Measuring Impact

Measure clarification time and proportion of orders with confirmed need, together with subsequent misunderstandings and changed scope.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
