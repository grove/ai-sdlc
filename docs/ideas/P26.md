# P26 Discover overlap between tools and solutions

<a id="p26"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Costs, risk and prioritization](../stages/problem.md#p-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Packaged Software, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can compare functions, purposes of use and operating costs in the tool portfolio to find possible double coverage. It can describe where overlap creates costs or information breaches, and what needs to be investigated before coordination or phasing out is a real option.

## Inputs and Evidence

Tool register, approved usage data, contract terms, costs, integrations and the teams' rationale for using the solutions in question.

## Prerequisites

Updated portfolio register, actual usage insight and access to contract and service owners for the affected solutions.

## Example and Deliverable

Several teams use different tools for the same test task. AI creates an overlap map with usage situations, dependencies and questions about functional differences before any coordination.

## Safeguards and Review

Utility owners confirm differences, exit costs and contract requirements; Similar product description does not mean that the solutions are practically interchangeable.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to validated overlap, along with documented costs, critical functional gaps and unnecessary consolidation proposals.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
