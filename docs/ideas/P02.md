# P02 Finding needs and contradictions in interviews

<a id="p02"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [User needs and business problems](../stages/problem.md#p-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Documents and Decisions, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can compile interviews and separate observed actions, expressed wishes and the analyst's interpretation. It can highlight where the informants agree or disagree, and suggest questions that clarify needs before the team chooses a specific solution.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved interview notes or transcripts, selection criteria, interview guide and description of the relevant target group.

## Prerequisites

Clarified treatment basis, safe transcription and access to professionals who can check the interpretation.

## Example and Deliverable

Interviews with case managers show both a need for fewer clicks and a better overview. AI creates an insight note with quotation sources and open follow-up questions.

## Safeguards and Review

A qualified analyst checks citations, interpretations, and sample limitations; AI shall not invent user statements.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time for quality-assured insight notes, together with erroneous interpretations and the need for new interviews.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
