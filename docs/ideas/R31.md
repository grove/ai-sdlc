# R31 Prepare controlled decommissioning of old solution

<a id="r31"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Controlled error handling and termination](../stages/production.md#r-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Legacy Systems, Maintenance, Data and Analytics

## Opportunity

AI can gather dependencies, active consumers, data considerations and contractual assumptions in a winding-up plan. It marks what must be confirmed before services or resources are removed. This helps projects realize planned simplifications and cost reductions after the replacement has been put into use.

## Inputs and Evidence

Service register, documented consumer use, data rules, agreements, migration status, cost overview and criteria for ending support for the old solution.

## Prerequisites

Compensation and consumer transfer must be verified, with clarified data, agreement and conservation obligations before resources can be removed.

## Example and Deliverable

An old report warehouse receives a decommissioning package that shows confirmed consumer transition, necessary archive, stoppage of jobs and responsible approver for final resource removal.

## Safeguards and Review

The service and data owner approves each irreversible step. Absence of recorded traffic alone does not prove that no one needs the solution anymore.

## Measuring Impact

Time to complete approved decommissioning and actually removed operating costs, along with lost dependencies, breach of preservation requirements and reopening needs.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
