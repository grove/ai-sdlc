# P24 Link field errors to equipment and operating conditions

<a id="p24"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Technology data and operation](../stages/problem.md#p-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Embedded and OT, Mobile and Client Software, Maintenance, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can compile error reports from installed equipment, firmware versions and environmental conditions to find problem candidates that do not occur in the laboratory. It can highlight differences between equipment groups and what needs to be investigated further with safe and controlled testing.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved field reports, equipment versions, firmware, operating conditions, maintenance history and metadata about how the failures were actually recorded.

## Prerequisites

Traceable link between equipment and version, sufficient field data and clarified responsibility for safe testing.

## Example and Deliverable

Sensors lose communication only in some installations after updating. AI creates a comparison overview of equipment, versions and conditions that require further testing.

## Safeguards and Review

Responsible engineer verifies data quality and possible security impact; the pattern allows for research hypotheses, not permission for automatic field changes.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to reproducible problem, along with failure classification, translated safety conditions and need for additional field visits.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
