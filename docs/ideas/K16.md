# K16 Create a prioritized clarification list

<a id="k16"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Requirements and task understanding](../stages/context.md#k-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Data and Analytics, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can find missing information and rank questions according to how much they block work or increase risk. It can distinguish questions that require human decision from information that can be obtained from known sources before execution begins.

## Inputs and Evidence

Task description, existing context, source catalog, known uncertainty and responsible roles.

## Prerequisites

An available clarification channel and agreed criteria for when ambiguity should stop execution.

## Example and Deliverable

For a data migration, AI delivers five prioritized questions about key duplicates, interruptions and reconciliation, with a proposed owner and the consequence of leaving the question open.

## Safeguards and Review

Subject manager confirms priority; the agent stops at critical ambiguities and does not fill blanks with probable answers.

## Measuring Impact

Time spent blocked by clarifications, along with questions without decision value and errors from hidden assumptions.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
