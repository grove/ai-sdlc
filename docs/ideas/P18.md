# P18 Link recurring operational problems to common patterns

<a id="p18"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Technology data and operation](../stages/problem.md#p-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Platform Engineering, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can structure incident reports and compare symptoms, affected services and previous measures. It can find recurring problem candidates across single incidents, allowing the team to examine lasting improvements and discover interventions that only mitigated symptoms.

## Inputs and Evidence

Incident reports, timelines, service maps, logs with approved data content and descriptions of measures that have already been attempted.

## Prerequisites

Access to quality-assured incident data, service ownership and a common definition of symptoms and causes.

## Example and Deliverable

Several nightly alarms concern the same queue which is filled after batch execution. AI creates a comprehensive problem card with a timeline and documented similarities.

## Safeguards and Review

Service Owner confirms event links and technical explanation; Similar wording or timing is not sufficient evidence of causation on its own.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to confirmed recurring problem, along with event recurrence and incorrectly merged events in the analysis.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
