# K06 Collect external contracts and supplier terms

<a id="k06"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [System and domain](../stages/context.md#k-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Packaged Software, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can retrieve relevant parts of approved supplier documentation and describe interfaces, quotas, compatibility and technical limitations. This gives the implementation agent a defined basis and makes requirements that are otherwise easily discovered late in the development visible.

## Inputs and Evidence

Authorized API descriptions, agreements, version information, quotas, support periods and integration needs.

## Prerequisites

Approved access to supplier sources and clarified right to process the material with the selected AI solution.

## Example and Deliverable

Before a payment integration, AI collects fields, error codes, authentication flow and test environment restrictions in a contract package with source version and questions to the supplier.

## Safeguards and Review

Integration owner checks current documentation; legally responsible interprets contract terms, and secrets are not included in the context package.

## Measuring Impact

Time to clarify the supporting evidence for integration, balanced with contract errors, outdated requirements and additional supplier clarifications.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
