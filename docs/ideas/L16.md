# L16 Improve when the agent should refrain and ask

<a id="l16"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Maintain knowledge and competence](../stages/learning.md#l-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Documents and Decisions, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can analyze errors, escalations and human takeovers to propose clearer rules for stopping and clarifying. The team can thus train on the right line between continuing, obtaining more context and leaving the task to a professional, without relying on the agent's own security score.

## Inputs and Evidence

Validated errors, clarification questions, acceptable answers, unresolved situations and registered takeovers.

## Prerequisites

Representative ambiguous tasks and an agreed recipient for escalation and professional clarification.

## Example and Deliverable

An agent guesses when a contract is unclear. AI creates an exercise collection with expected stops, precise clarification questions and approved examples of continuation.

## Safeguards and Review

Subject owner validates the stopping rules against representative tasks. The model's linguistic confidence or own score is not a reliable proof of correctness.

## Measuring Impact

Incorrect continuation in case of ambiguity and unnecessary stops, together with total time to correct task solution.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
