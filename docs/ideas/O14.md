# O14 Observe handovers and clarification work

<a id="o14"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Delivery flow and resource use](../stages/observation.md#o-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Regulated Systems, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can analyze case history and approved collaboration data to describe how often work is sent between roles or is left pending clarification. It distinguishes necessary professional checks from unnecessary rounds due to a lack of context or unclear ownership.

## Inputs and Evidence

Handover events, clarification categories, responsibility maps and de-identified team notes with agreed purpose of use.

## Prerequisites

Common understanding of handover and permission for purpose-limited use of collaboration data.

## Example and Deliverable

An integration change goes between three teams. AI creates a handover map with waiting time, unanswered questions and specific examples of missing context.

## Safeguards and Review

The teams check the incident understanding and necessary control points. Do not optimize away independent professional approval.

## Measuring Impact

Waiting time and number of unnecessary clarification rounds, with errors during handover and necessary control maintained.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
