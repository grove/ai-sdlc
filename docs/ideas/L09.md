# L09 Update the team's work instructions

<a id="l09"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Maintain knowledge and competence](../stages/learning.md#l-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Integrations, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can convert documented experiences into short, reusable work instructions with purpose, assumptions, examples and controls. A good recipe also describes when it doesn't fit, so that other teams can adapt the practice instead of copying it mechanically.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved learning records, completed tasks, known deviations and existing job descriptions.

## Prerequisites

At least one verified experience and an owner who maintains the recipe.

## Example and Deliverable

A team succeeds in contract tests. AI writes a recipe that shows the necessary API descriptions, test oracles, checkpoints and an initial task.

## Safeguards and Review

Subject owner tests the recipe for new work before publication. Preserve version, sources and known limitations.

## Measuring Impact

Time to approved completion of the recipe, with deviations, rework and errors during reuse.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
