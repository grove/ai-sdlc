# K27 Adapting language without changing academic meaning

<a id="k27"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Maintenance and reuse of context](../stages/context.md#k-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Packaged Software, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can translate and explain context between languages and professional environments, while retaining the term list and original source. This can reduce collaboration friction in distributed teams and make requirements understandable for both developers, users and subject specialists.

## Inputs and Evidence

Original text, approved list of terms, target group, desired language and particularly important subject terms.

## Prerequisites

A clarified authoritative source and competence to check subject expressions in both languages.

## Example and Deliverable

A English set of requirements is transformed into an English work context for a supplier team, with conceptual links and marking of formulations that cannot be translated unambiguously.

## Safeguards and Review

Professional checks critical importance; the authoritative original and clarified translations must be comparable.

## Measuring Impact

Clarification rounds between specialist communities, together with translation errors and reworking from changed meaning in requirements.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
