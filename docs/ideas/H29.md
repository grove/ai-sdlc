# H29 Formulate effect tests for deliveries without a code

<a id="h29"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Risk and decision rules](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Regulated Systems, Maintenance, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can help the project to define what use and benefit mean for architectural choice, documentation, requirements or decision-making basis. It can suggest observable criteria such as fewer clarifications or better decision support, so that non-coding work can also be assessed on more than production speed.

## Inputs and Evidence

The delivery's target group, task of use, existing quality requirements, previous questions and a baseline for how corresponding artefacts are used in practice.

## Prerequisites

Identified recipients, realistic usage scenarios and access to independent professional assessment of the information provided.

## Example and Deliverable

An AI-generated operating guide is tested by new operating supervisors on representative scenarios. AI creates a hypothesis and evaluation plan for understanding, task performance and academic errors.

## Safeguards and Review

Recipient and subject owner approve usage criteria; readable text and fast production should not alone be equated with good decision-making quality.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to testable utility hypothesis, together with users' task performance, factual errors and necessary subsequent clarification work.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
