# H13 Assess data needs before the experiment starts

<a id="h13"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Experiments and measurement baseline](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Data and Analytics, Product Development, Regulated Systems, Embedded and OT

## Opportunity

AI can help an analyst formulate the minimum meaningful effect, map variation and assess which data base is needed. It can also point out when an experiment is likely to only provide exploratory insight, so that the project avoids drawing firm conclusions from a few tasks.

## Inputs and Evidence

Historical distributions, expected volume, desired decision, minimum useful effect and relevant dependencies between observations in the working committee.

## Prerequisites

Historical observations or pilot data, relevant analytical skills and a clarified threshold for what is useful for decision-making.

## Example and Deliverable

A team delivers few major changes per month. AI creates a data requirements note with possible measurement windows and limitations when interpreting small samples.

## Safeguards and Review

Analyst checks calculations and method in suitable tools; AI-generated numbers must not be accepted without controlled assumptions.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to realistic data needs, together with analysis errors, completed trials without a basis for decision-making and unnecessarily long measurements.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
