# R01 Create an overall basis for the release decision

<a id="r01"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Preparation and release decision](../stages/production.md#r-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Regulated Systems, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can compile the status of tests, deviations, approvals and operational readiness in one release assessment. It marks missing information and which conditions remain. This reduces manual coordination when technically completed changes must be assessed together before they are made available to the recipients in production.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved building, test certificate, deviation register, release requirements, service owner, operating plan and actual status from the authoritative systems.

## Prerequisites

Release requirements and approval roles must be clarified, and status must be able to be retrieved from updated and authoritative sources.

## Example and Deliverable

For a monthly release, AI creates a decision report showing unresolved data migration and missing duty officer, with links to underlying evidence and owners.

## Safeguards and Review

The release manager makes the decision. The report must distinguish confirmed status from assumptions, and unfulfilled mandatory conditions must still block rollout.

## Measuring Impact

Time from verified change to release decision, together with deliveries that are stopped late due to missing or incorrect information.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
