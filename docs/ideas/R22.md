# R22 Clarify capacity and cost limits at launch

<a id="r22"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Operational readiness and reception](../stages/production.md#r-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, AI in the product, Data and Analytics

## Opportunity

AI can compare load tests, expected use and price or capacity data to design an operational start-up budget. It proposes limits, warnings and responsibilities for increasing resources. This supports practical operational readiness and makes financial assumptions visible before traffic or model use is activated.

## Inputs and Evidence

Verified load results, expected volumes, documented prices, resource quotas, budget limits and clarified owners of operations and costs.

## Prerequisites

The team needs measured resource needs and current cost data, as well as authorization and mechanisms to establish quotas and alerts.

## Example and Deliverable

A AI service receives a launch plan with an approved call quota, maximum concurrency level and notification in case of consumption, based on measured costs from representative tasks.

## Safeguards and Review

The platform and finance manager approves the framework. Prices and traffic conditions must be verified, and hard restrictions must be assessed against accessibility requirements.

## Measuring Impact

Time to clarify the operating budget, together with unforeseen cost overruns, quota errors and user interruptions due to incorrect dimensioning at start-up.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
