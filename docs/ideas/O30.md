# O30 Catch rare and delayed errors

<a id="o30"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Credible impact analysis](../stages/observation.md#o-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Embedded and OT, Regulated Systems, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can collect deviations that occur long after release, and follow whether they belong to previous changes or known risk categories. This is useful when a short observation time can otherwise give a misleading picture of quality, especially at the change of month, unusual load patterns or rare units.

## Inputs and Evidence

Long-term error history, versions, operational calendar, load patterns and relevant device or data categories.

## Prerequisites

Sufficient observation horizon and identifiable version in events over time.

## Example and Deliverable

A calculation run only fails at the end of the period. AI links confirmed errors to previous releases and updates the quality follow-up with traceable evidence.

## Safeguards and Review

The specialist confirms connection and consequence. Absence of observed errors is not evidence that rare errors have been ruled out.

## Measuring Impact

Time to identified delayed fault and severity per exposure, with observation time and coverage gap.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
