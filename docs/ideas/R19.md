# R19 Carry out a clear handover to the service owner

<a id="r19"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Operational readiness and reception](../stages/production.md#r-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Regulated Systems, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can structure the handover between project and administration around responsibility, access, knowledge and outstanding work. It collects agreed tasks and marks unclear ownership before the project ends. This reduces the risk that a delivered solution lacks someone who can actually maintain and support it.

## Inputs and Evidence

Service description, responsibility matrix, operating agreements, deviation register, support channels, maintenance plan and confirmations from the roles that will receive the solution.

## Prerequisites

The recipient and lines of responsibility must be identified, and the handover requirements must include both technical operation and necessary professional management.

## Example and Deliverable

For a new data service, AI creates a handover protocol with ownership of data quality, access ordering, incident management and deadlines for known technical debt.

## Safeguards and Review

The receiving service owner accepts responsibility explicitly. AI shall not interpret non-response as approval or transfer responsibility automatically.

## Measuring Impact

Time to complete handover, together with unresolved areas of responsibility, cases that fall between teams and lack of access when first support is needed.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
