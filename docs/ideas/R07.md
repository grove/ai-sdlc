# R07 Check that the correct approval follows the correct delivery

<a id="r07"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Preparation and release decision](../stages/production.md#r-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Regulated Systems, Maintenance, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can compare the release's identity, risk class and recipient with the applicable approval rules. It may reveal expired exemptions or approvals that apply to a different version. This reduces manual administrative work and prevents a technically approved change from being confused with formal permission to start production.

## Inputs and Evidence

Release identity, risk class, authorization rules, time-limited exceptions, signed approvals and decisions from the organization's established change process.

## Prerequisites

The approval rules must be explicit and linked to stable delivery identities, with traceable roles and validity periods.

## Example and Deliverable

A release draft receives a deviation report because the risk acceptance applies to the previous build, while the new version also changes data storage and requires new processing.

## Safeguards and Review

Authorized people understand the approvals. Deterministic gateways verify identity and access, and AI should never create or replace formal signatures.

## Measuring Impact

Time spent on administrative release control, along with incorrect approval links and deployments lacking valid authorization.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
