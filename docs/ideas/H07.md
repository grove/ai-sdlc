# H07 Compare architecture choices against specific scenarios

<a id="h07"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Alternatives and mode of operation](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Platform Engineering, Legacy Systems, Embedded and OT

## Opportunity

AI can formulate architecture alternatives and assess them against predefined change, operational and failure scenarios. It can make trade-offs clear and suggest which technical tests should be carried out, so that the decision is based on the project's needs rather than general architectural preferences.

## Inputs and Evidence

Functional requirements, quality requirements, architectural limits, load expectations, expertise and relevant scenarios for maintenance, errors and further development.

## Prerequisites

Explicit quality requirements, an overview of existing system limits and professionals who can assess operating and change scenarios.

## Example and Deliverable

The team is considering synchronous API or event flow for order processing. AI prepares an ADR draft with scenarios, uncertain conditions and proposed technical clarifications before elections.

## Safeguards and Review

Architect and business owner validate realism, dependencies and security; performance and cost claims require measurement or verified sources.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time for justified architectural choice, together with translated quality requirements, trial cost and need for later re-selection.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
