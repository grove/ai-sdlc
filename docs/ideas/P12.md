# P12 Make interruptions and hidden work visible

<a id="p12"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Bottlenecks and workflow](../stages/problem.md#p-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Platform Engineering, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can categorize voluntary work samples and registered urgent tasks to describe work that does not appear in the delivery plan. It can distinguish necessary interruptions from unnecessary switching between tasks and suggest which patterns the team should investigate further.

## Inputs and Evidence

Representative work samples, incidents, support tasks, the team's own descriptions and rules for using working environment data.

## Prerequisites

Voluntary or otherwise clarified data collection, team aggregates and sufficient breadth in the working committee.

## Example and Deliverable

Planned development is often interrupted by manual account creation. AI creates a comprehensive overview of interruption types, time estimates and cases that lack a clear owner.

## Safeguards and Review

The team validates categories and representativeness; individual messages or activity tracks shall not be used as covert time recording.

## Measuring Impact

Measure mapping time and proportion of visible work, together with team experience, error categorization and cost of registration.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
