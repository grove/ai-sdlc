# H08 Consider building, buying or reusing

<a id="h08"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Alternatives and mode of operation](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Packaged Software, Platform Engineering, AI in the product, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can structure alternatives for in-house development, existing platform and supplier solution with the same functional needs as a starting point. It can collect assumptions about total cost, integration, competence and exit, so that the procurement choice becomes a verifiable decision basis rather than a function list.

## Inputs and Evidence

Requirements, internal capabilities, current offers, verified supplier terms, integration needs and expected lifetime of the solution in the business.

## Prerequisites

Access to valid offers and terms, common requirements and owners for technical and commercial assessments.

## Example and Deliverable

The project assesses self-developed knowledge search against existing platform functions. AI supplies a comparison note with functional gaps, cost assumptions and the need for a demonstration or pilot before acquisition.

## Safeguards and Review

Purchasing, the technical owner and the legal responsible control the current conditions; AI shall not invent prices or contract rights.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time for complete alternative assessment, together with missing cost items, verified functional gaps and later change costs.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
