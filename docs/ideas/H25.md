# H25 Carry out a systematic analysis of possible failed pilots

<a id="h25"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Risk and decision rules](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, AI in the product, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can start from the fact that the experiment has failed and propose plausible explanations such as missing data, low suitability for the task or increased control work. It can translate these into observable signals and preventive measures, so that the team detects weaknesses before they start.

## Inputs and Evidence

Pilot plan, expected value, known limitations, risk log and experiences from similar changes in the team's way of working or technology.

## Prerequisites

A concrete pilot plan, participation from various professional roles and a willingness to change the experiment after critical assessment.

## Example and Deliverable

A pilot is believed to have provided more drafts but slower delivery. AI creates a preliminary analysis with possible mechanisms, early signals and responsible control points.

## Safeguards and Review

The team prioritizes realistic scenarios and checks them against sources; long lists of hypothetical hazards should not block low-risk learning.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to identified pilot weaknesses, together with relevant warning signals, unnecessary checks and later unexpected extra work.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
