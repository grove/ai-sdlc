# P01 Collect recurring issues from feedback

<a id="p01"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [User needs and business problems](../stages/problem.md#p-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Maintenance, Mobile and Client Software, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can group support cases, product reviews and internal inquiries according to the problem the sender describes. It can collect alternative formulations and display representative sources, so that the team can more easily distinguish widespread problems from many copies of the same single incident.

## Inputs and Evidence

Feedback with date, channel, product area and known links between cases or events.

## Prerequisites

Legal access to the sources, removal of unnecessary personal data and a common problem taxonomy.

## Example and Deliverable

Three hundred inquiries about logging in are grouped into expired links, unclear error messages and lack of access. The delivery is a problem map with traceable examples.

## Safeguards and Review

The product owner reviews representative samples and separates the number of inquiries from the number of affected users before final prioritization.

## Measuring Impact

Measure analysis time per volume of records, together with classification accuracy and proportion of problems confirmed through user insight.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
