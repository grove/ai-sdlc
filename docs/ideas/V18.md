# V18 Verify that reports express correct business terms

<a id="v18"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Specialized deliveries](../stages/verification.md#v-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Data and Analytics, Packaged Software, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can compare definitions of indicators with queries, filters and visualizations in reports. It marks semantic differences such as mixing orders and customers or different time windows. This can reduce erroneous decisions that occur even if the data platform and calculations technically work.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved term catalog, report requirements, queries, filter rules, update frequency and authoritative reference numbers for limited examples.

## Prerequisites

The business must have clarified concepts and reference bases, with a named owner who can accept the calculation rules.

## Example and Deliverable

For a sales dashboard, AI delivers a control report that shows that gross and net sales are mixed, with reconciliation against an approved sample account.

## Safeguards and Review

Subject owner determines definitions and coordination. AI must show which figures and rules it is based on, and unexplained deviations block approval.

## Measuring Impact

Time for professionally approved report, together with reconciliation deviations and corrections required after the report has been put into use.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
