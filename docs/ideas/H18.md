# H18 Investigate sensitivity in the utility calculation

<a id="h18"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Economy capacity and working methods](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Product Development, AI in the product, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can propose relevant scenarios and calculate how the net benefit changes when volume, acceptance rate, control needs or price vary. It can identify assumptions that determine the investment, so that the project knows which uncertain figures should be measured most carefully.

## Inputs and Evidence

Controlled cost model, documented intervals, historical variation and the assumptions that can change whether the measure provides positive net value.

## Prerequisites

A reviewed calculation, justified variation intervals and a suitable calculation tool with verifiable formulas and assumptions.

## Example and Deliverable

The profitability of a coding agent depends on extra review time. AI creates a sensitivity analysis with tipping points and suggests which work samples should be collected in the pilot.

## Safeguards and Review

Analyst controls the calculations and intervals; constructed probabilities should not give a false impression of precision.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to clarified tipping points, together with calculation errors and correspondence between assumed and later observed variations.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
