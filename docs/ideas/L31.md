# L31 Check that learning is actually applied

<a id="l31"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Scale and manage good working practices](../stages/learning.md#l-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Product Development, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can follow decisions from retrospectives to a changed template, test, context or workflow, and on to a new observation. It shows which measures have been closed administratively, but have not yet had a verified effect, so that the improvement work has a clear conclusion.

## Inputs and Evidence

Learning records, initiative owners, change history, verification and subsequent measurements.

## Prerequisites

Traceable link from decision to concrete change and planned follow-up.

## Example and Deliverable

A team decided to improve acceptance criteria. AI creates a follow-up overview that shows the new template, actual use and whether the clarification work went down.

## Safeguards and Review

Initiative owner confirms implementation and effect. AI must not close an initiative because documentation exists or the deadline has passed.

## Measuring Impact

Proportion of learning measures with observed effect and time to effect, with quality of the evidence base.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
