# K09 Link requirements to specific acceptance criteria

<a id="k09"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Requirements and task understanding](../stages/context.md#k-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Regulated Systems, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can transform broad needs into proposals for observable acceptance criteria and link them to original requirements. It makes the task easier to carry out and gives the verification a clear basis without the agent having to guess the desired result.

## Inputs and Evidence

Accepted requirements, user stories, business rules, current interfaces and examples of expected behavior.

## Prerequisites

A claims manager capable of making decisions and a clear distinction between confirmed claims and assumptions.

## Example and Deliverable

A vague history of faster search is transformed into criteria for relevant results, error handling and measurement under specified load, with open target values for the product owner.

## Safeguards and Review

Product owner and professional approve the criteria; AI must not invent performance limits or expand the scope without a decision.

## Measuring Impact

Clarifications during execution and time to approved delivery, checked against acceptance deviations and overlooked functionality.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
