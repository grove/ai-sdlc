# R03 Write release information for various recipients

<a id="r03"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Preparation and release decision](../stages/production.md#r-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Documents and Decisions, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can transform approved changes into precise information for end users, operations, support functions and integration partners. It distinguishes between what has been changed, who is affected and what the recipient must do. This can reduce the work of explaining the same release at several relevant levels.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved list of changes, target groups, known limitations, migration needs, help texts and clarified time for making functionality available.

## Prerequisites

There must be an approved factual basis, clear target groups and responsible persons who can approve information before publication.

## Example and Deliverable

For a new login method, AI creates a user letter, operating note and partner information with different details, but the same confirmed changes and action deadlines.

## Safeguards and Review

The product owner or communications manager approves content and recipients before sending. AI must not claim that a function is active before activation.

## Measuring Impact

Time for approved release communication, together with incorrect information, the recipients' need for clarification and support inquiries due to misunderstandings during the introduction.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
