# L22 Adapt autonomy to documented experiences

<a id="l22"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Improve agents and work tools](../stages/learning.md#l-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Regulated Systems, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can summarize which tasks are consistently successful within the mandate, and where escalation or errors are still common. It proposes narrower or more precise powers, so that the degree of execution is developed from evidence of consequence and control needs.

## Inputs and Evidence

Driving outcomes, risk classes, takeovers, policy deviations, independent verifications and task types.

## Prerequisites

Sufficient experience in the relevant task class and independent access enforcement.

## Example and Deliverable

An agent succeeds with documentation fixes, but struggles with migrations. AI proposes different mandates and control points for the two task classes.

## Safeguards and Review

The risk owner approves each access change. Historical success alone does not justify production access or removal of required approvals.

## Measuring Impact

Correct completion within mandate, with serious deviations, control cost and human takeover.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
