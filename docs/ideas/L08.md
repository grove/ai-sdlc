# L08 Weigh short-term gain against maintenance work

<a id="l08"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Improve hypotheses and prioritization](../stages/learning.md#l-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Legacy Systems, Product Development, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can link pilot gains to later changes, understanding problems and technical debt. It helps the team revise usage rules when fast delivery creates greater future cost, and suggests which parts of the way of working should be kept or simplified.

## Inputs and Evidence

Long-term observations, maintenance cases, code reviews, architecture decisions and changed cost picture.

## Prerequisites

Planned long-term follow-up and access to later work on the same components.

## Example and Deliverable

Generated modules were quick to create but difficult to modify. AI proposes a more limited pattern with standard components and simpler structure.

## Safeguards and Review

Technical owner assesses actual maintenance certificates. Estimated future debt must be marked as assumption and tested against later work.

## Measuring Impact

Total cost over agreed follow-up periods, with ease of change and error rate in affected solution.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
