# P30 Map repetitive operational work

<a id="p30"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Costs, risk and prioritization](../stages/problem.md#p-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Platform Engineering, Data and Analytics

## Opportunity

AI can analyze runbooks, events and recorded operations to identify manual tasks that are repeated with similar assumptions. It can describe variations, risks and exceptions, so that the team gets good problem candidates before automation or agent-based execution is considered.

## Inputs and Evidence

Runbooks, work logs, change requests, error history and documented exceptions for repeated tasks in service operation and platform management.

## Prerequisites

Access to approved operational documentation, representative operational logs and a service owner who can validate the assumptions.

## Example and Deliverable

Operators restart the same job after a known queue error. AI creates a problem card with frequency, manual effort, exceptions and consequences.

## Safeguards and Review

The operator confirms repeatability and cause; stable manual procedure is not automatically safe to delegate to an agent.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to bounded operational problem, along with repetitive manual work, incidents and translate operational exceptions.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
