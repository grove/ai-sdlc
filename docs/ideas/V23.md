# V23 Plan tests across client variants

<a id="v23"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Specialized deliveries](../stages/verification.md#v-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Mobile and Client Software, Product Development, Embedded and OT

## Opportunity

AI can prioritize combinations of devices, operating systems, networks and client versions that should be tested. It compares support policy with the technical consequences of the change. This can reduce manual planning of large test arrays while still covering critical processes on older or limited clients.

## Inputs and Evidence

Supported client versions, device distribution, changed interfaces, known compatibility issues, and offline or unstable network connection requirements.

## Prerequisites

A clear support policy and access to representative devices or emulators must be found before test coverage can be prioritized.

## Example and Deliverable

For a mobile app, AI creates a test matrix with upgrade from previous version, poor connection during save and reopening after the operating system's process exit.

## Safeguards and Review

Product and test manager approve the coverage. Rare but mandatory client groups must not disappear because the usage data shows a low proportion.

## Measuring Impact

Time to approved client test plan, along with critical untested combinations and confirmed client bugs after deployed update.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
