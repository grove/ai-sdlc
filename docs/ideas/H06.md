# H06 Design the smallest useful experiment

<a id="h06"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Alternatives and mode of operation](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, AI in the product, Platform Engineering, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can propose a limited trial that examines a critical uncertainty with the least possible implementation. It can compare manual simulation, prototype, historical task test and pilot in actual work, so that the experiment provides relevant learning before major investments.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hypothesis, critical uncertainty, acceptable risk, representative tasks and available environments or people for a limited experiment.

## Prerequisites

Clear research question, representative examples and opportunity to refine the experiment without changing its purpose.

## Example and Deliverable

Before a complete code agent is built, the same task contract is tested on a small sample of historical errors. AI supplies the experimental plan, data requirements and criteria for further work.

## Safeguards and Review

The technical and product manager confirms that the experiment examines the correct uncertainty; a neat demonstration is not documentation of production effect.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time and cost to decision-making learning, along with transferability and subsequent fallacies from simplified trials.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
