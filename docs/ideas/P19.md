# P19 Find costly and slow usage processes

<a id="p19"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Technology data and operation](../stages/problem.md#p-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Data and Analytics, AI in the product

## Opportunity

AI can compare profiles, tracking data and resource costs to identify operations that are both slow or expensive and important to users. It can suggest which problems merit further measurement, without inferring cause directly from covariation.

## Inputs and Evidence

Profiles, aggregated traces, cost distribution, user tasks and known limits for what the measurement data actually covers in the system.

## Prerequisites

Relationship between service, cost and user task as well as measurement data from representative load and time periods.

## Example and Deliverable

An export uses a lot of memory and creates a wait at period closing. AI creates a prioritized problem picture with affected processes and proposals for verification.

## Safeguards and Review

Developer and service owner check the measurements, normalize for load and confirm user significance before technical reasons are concluded.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to validated performance issue, along with cost per issue, user experience, and incorrect issue recommendations.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
