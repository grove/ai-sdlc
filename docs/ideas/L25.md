# L25 Find experiences that can be reused by other teams

<a id="l25"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Scale and manage good working practices](../stages/learning.md#l-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Product Development, Data and Analytics

## Opportunity

AI can compare learning records and suggest a match between a team's concrete problem and another team's documented experience. It shows similarities, differences and necessary prerequisites, so that reuse becomes a targeted local trial rather than a broad roll-out on a thin basis.

## Inputs and Evidence

Access approved learning registers, problem profiles, technology stacks, risk classes and effect bases.

## Prerequisites

Searchable learning records with enough context to assess transferability.

## Example and Deliverable

A new team is waiting for test data. AI finds a previous attempt with synthetic data and creates a transfer note with differences in privacy and domain.

## Safeguards and Review

Both subject holders assess relevance and access. Results shall not be used for competition ranking between different teams.

## Measuring Impact

Time for a relevant local trial, with realized local effect and adaptation cost as control measures.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
