# K13 Map data flow and privacy needs

<a id="k13"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [Requirements and task understanding](../stages/context.md#k-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Data and Analytics, Regulated Systems, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can propose which data is processed, where it flows, and which restrictions follow from approved internal rules. The map helps the team choose data minimization and test bases, while also highlighting legal clarifications before execution.

## Inputs and Evidence

Data models, service links, processing overviews, storage rules and approved examples without unnecessary personal data.

## Prerequisites

Data sources that can be analyzed within approved processing and identifiable owners for data and purposes.

## Example and Deliverable

Before a customer report is built, AI creates a data flow map showing fields, recipients, storage and unresolved questions about purpose and deletion.

## Safeguards and Review

Privacy and data owner confirms classification and legal use; AI does not determine the grounds for processing or legal obligations.

## Measuring Impact

Time to clarify the supporting data, supplemented by unnecessary fields, misclassification and privacy discrepancies discovered later.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
