# L14 Create training from the team's own experiences

<a id="l14"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Maintain knowledge and competence](../stages/learning.md#l-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Legacy Systems, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can turn approved tasks, mistakes and decisions into short exercises that train problem formulation, control and context use. The material can show several plausible answers and reasons, so that the training develops assessment skills rather than just knowledge of tool buttons.

## Inputs and Evidence

Verified examples, learning objectives, work instructions, anonymised errors and professionally approved solutions.

## Prerequisites

Clear competence targets and professionals who can check the training material.

## Example and Deliverable

New developers train on a previous integration failure. AI creates an exercise with a source pack, unclear assumptions and an assessment guide for the mentor.

## Safeguards and Review

Subject owner approves tasks and answers. Remove sensitive data and do not allow AI to produce unvalidated findings.

## Measuring Impact

Time for independent, correct task completion, with quality justifications and necessary mentor support.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
