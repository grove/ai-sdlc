# P13 Finding patterns in rework and reopened cases

<a id="p13"></a>

- **Stage:** [Problem](../stages/problem.md)
- **Category:** [Bottlenecks and workflow](../stages/problem.md#p-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Legacy Systems, Product Development, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can link reopened tasks, corrective changes, and error reports to likely categories of rework. It can show whether the patterns point to unclear requirements, insufficient testing or coordination, so that the team investigates the causes systematically instead of single incidents.

## Inputs and Evidence

Case history, change links, error reports, acceptance criteria and explanations from those who carried out the corrections in question.

## Prerequisites

Reliable connection between cases and corrections, defined error classes and the possibility of professional verification.

## Example and Deliverable

Many integration cases are reopened because zero values have not been clarified. AI supplies a pattern map with examples and questions for requirements work and test practice.

## Safeguards and Review

The specialist confirms cause categories; Coincidence between a working method and rework is not proof of cause alone.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to confirmed rework pattern, along with repeated errors, rework rate and inaccurate cause classification.

## Applying This Idea

Use the idea to define a problem with sources, consequence and responsible owner. Also follow up on whether the completed problem basis later leads to relevant work and better outcomes.
