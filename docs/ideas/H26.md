# H26 Design a reversible and limited trial

<a id="h26"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Risk and decision rules](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, Product Development, Maintenance, Packaged Software

## Opportunity

AI can propose how the measure is tested with a limited scope, clear exposure and a known way back. It can describe which data, working methods and accesses are affected, so that the team can test an idea without committing the entire project to the solution.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hypothesis, system limits, risk class, rollback possibilities, data flow and dependencies that can make the experiment difficult to reverse or limit.

## Prerequisites

Technical possibility for isolation, known starting point and ownership to stop or reverse the experiment if necessary.

## Example and Deliverable

An agent is first tested on draft documentation in a separate work area. AI creates a pilot plan with stoppage, return and transfer to ordinary working methods.

## Safeguards and Review

Service Owner verifies that rollback actually works; data exposure and irreversible external actions cannot be made reversible only through a plan.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to controlled pilot design, along with extent of exposure, payback time and unplanned impact on regular work.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
