# V22 Prepare verification of embedded systems

<a id="v22"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Specialized deliveries](../stages/verification.md#v-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Embedded and OT, Regulated Systems, Integrations

## Opportunity

AI can connect real-time requirements, power usage and fault conditions with tests in simulator or hardware. It can explain log deviations and suggest situations that are missing from the test matrix. This supports the specialists when software behavior depends on physical signals and limitations in actual equipment.

## Inputs and Evidence

Hardware specification, time requirements, interface, traces from simulator or test rig and approved scenarios for abnormal signals.

## Prerequisites

The team must have a controlled test rig, known hardware version and traceable requirements for physical behavior and response time.

## Example and Deliverable

For a sensor control, AI creates a matrix for power outages during storage, erroneous measurement values and deadlines, with links to results from the test rig.

## Safeguards and Review

Qualified professionals approve claims and evidence. AI does not replace necessary hardware tests, formal verification or certification activities in safety-critical systems.

## Measuring Impact

Time to complete verification matrix, along with missing hardware coverage, timing deviations and errors first detected on actual equipment.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
