# K04 Collect terms and rules in a common overview

<a id="k04"></a>

- **Stage:** [Context](../stages/context.md)
- **Category:** [System and domain](../stages/context.md#k-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Packaged Software, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can find different names for the same business term and collect definitions, rules and exceptions from approved sources. The overview reduces misunderstandings between developers, domain experts and agents when describing and implementing new changes.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved subject descriptions, forms, API fields, user stories and examples from the domain.

## Prerequisites

Named professional owners and access to sources that actually govern the company's rules.

## Example and Deliverable

For an insurance project, AI creates a term list that distinguishes customer, insured and payer, with definitions and marked cases where the sources use different names.

## Safeguards and Review

Domain experts approve meanings and exceptions; legal or economic definitions are determined by the appropriate professional role.

## Measuring Impact

Clarification rounds per task and rework from term errors, checked against technical errors in the overview.

## Applying This Idea

Agree which sources, access and quality requirements apply to the specific task. Measure both the time to usable context and rework due to deficiencies or errors in the foundation.
