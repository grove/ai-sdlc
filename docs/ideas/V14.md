# V14 Verify error handling in dependencies

<a id="v14"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Safety and robustness](../stages/verification.md#v-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Integrations, Platform Engineering, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can propose scenarios for unavailable services, timeouts, partial responses and repeated deliveries. It compares expected degradation with actual test results. This can make robustness testing more systematic and reveal when a local error has unexpected consequences in the rest of the supply chain.

## Inputs and Evidence

Dependency maps, recovery requirements, retry rules, timeouts, service contracts and approved opportunities for controlled error injection.

## Prerequisites

There must be observable end results, testable dependencies, and an approved plan to limit the consequences of misinjection.

## Example and Deliverable

A test plan examines whether orders are registered exactly once when the payment confirmation is delayed; AI supplies a list of deviations and documented final results from the experiment.

## Safeguards and Review

The operator approves the fault injection and the stop criteria. Evidence must show actual system behavior, and uncertain scenarios must be run outside of production.

## Measuring Impact

Confirmed pre-release error handling weaknesses, along with recovery time in controlled tests and lost or duplicate transactions.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
