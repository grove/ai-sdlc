# O11 Measure the cost of review and rework

<a id="o11"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Delivery flow and resource use](../stages/observation.md#o-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Legacy Systems, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can group review comments and reopened cases by error type, and collate them with actual work effort. It reveals when good first drafts are replaced by many small corrections, or when a quick agent delivery puts more work on other professional roles.

## Inputs and Evidence

Review comments, reopenings, representative time trials and link between tasks and agent use.

## Prerequisites

Representative effort measurement and consistent definitions of review, error correction and new functionality.

## Example and Deliverable

A team spends less time on implementation. AI makes an analysis of additional control work, semantic errors and rework after acceptance.

## Safeguards and Review

Professionals validate the error taxonomy. Comment volume is not working time, and origin should not be used for individual ranking.

## Measuring Impact

Total review and rework time per accepted change, with released errors and maintainability.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
