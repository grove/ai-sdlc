# R05 Suggest workable release windows

<a id="r05"></a>

- **Stage:** [Production](../stages/production.md)
- **Category:** [Preparation and release decision](../stages/production.md#r-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Maintenance, Regulated Systems, Platform Engineering

## Opportunity

AI can compare the change calendar, traffic patterns, staffing and dependencies to propose release windows with practical readiness. It can highlight conflicts and lack of capacity before the plan is locked. This reduces coordination time and the risk of a rollout taking place without the necessary professionals available in case of deviations.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved change calendar, resource and duty schedule, dependent activities, traffic profiles and requirements for support or availability during rollout.

## Prerequisites

Calendars and staffing data must be legally available, up-to-date and limited to the information actually needed for planning.

## Example and Deliverable

For a database migration, AI provides three possible time windows with conflicts, necessary roles and expected operational load, so that the release manager can choose and agree on implementation.

## Safeguards and Review

Responsible persons confirm availability and window. AI must not book people, notify external parties or change production plans without an express mandate.

## Measuring Impact

Time to the agreed release window, together with rescheduling, lack of staffing and executions that have to be interrupted due to known conflicts.

## Applying This Idea

Clarify what intended use means for the delivery, who approves the transfer and who owns the operation. Follow the introduction with measurements and a workable plan for failure.
