# H28 Plan samples of portability and vendor dependency

<a id="h28"></a>

- **Stage:** [Hypothesis](../stages/hypothesis.md)
- **Category:** [Risk and decision rules](../stages/hypothesis.md#h-category-4)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Platform Engineering, AI in the product, Packaged Software, Regulated Systems

## Opportunity

AI can propose a limited test of whether context, logs, evaluations and workflow can be moved between approved solutions. It can make exit conditions concrete, so that the project considers future replacement work together with the gains from today's tool selection before major investments.

## Inputs and Evidence

Tool architecture, export possibilities, applicable agreements, context formats, integrations and requirements for further operation if the supplier or model has to be changed.

## Prerequisites

Access to relevant products and agreements, testable export routes and criteria for which functions must be preserved.

## Example and Deliverable

The team is considering an agent platform. AI creates a trial plan for exporting task templates and results, rerunning evaluations and mapping manual adaptations.

## Safeguards and Review

Technical owner and purchasing confirm access and rights; documented export functionality is not proof of full functional portability.

## Measuring Impact

Measure time to proven exit basis, together with migration effort, loss of function and preservation of necessary traceability in the sample.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the idea into a limited experiment with hypothesis, basis for comparison and decision rules. Record the expected value, control needs and uncertainty before the results are available.
