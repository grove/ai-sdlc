# V01 Track claims to actual test evidence

<a id="v01"></a>

- **Stage:** [Verification](../stages/verification.md)
- **Category:** [Requirements and functional correctness](../stages/verification.md#v-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Drafting
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Regulated Systems, Documents and Decisions

## Opportunity

AI can link acceptance criteria to implementation, test cases and actual operational results. It marks claims without evidence and tests that only verify a small part of the claim. The team gets a more precise basis for deciding whether the change can be approved.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved requirements, change diff, test descriptions, results from controlled CI and previous clarifications with the product owner.

## Prerequisites

The requirements must have stable identifiers, clear criteria and available results from controlled test runs.

## Example and Deliverable

For a refund function, AI creates a traceability matrix showing evidence of amount limits, access, rounding and rejected transactions, with links to test runs.

## Safeguards and Review

The tester confirms the links and assesses the strength of the evidence. A link to a test is not automatically considered as documented compliance.

## Measuring Impact

Time for complete claim verification, together with the proportion of mandatory claims without valid evidence and errors discovered after acceptance.

## Applying This Idea

Agree what is a correct result and what independent evidence the approval requires. Measure the inspection effort together with errors that are detected, errors that are overlooked and unnecessary rejections.
