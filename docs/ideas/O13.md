# O13 Follow the developers' perceived friction

<a id="o13"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Delivery flow and resource use](../stages/observation.md#o-category-2)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Product Development, Platform Engineering, Maintenance

## Opportunity

AI can summarize voluntary team surveys and retrospective input on interruptions, uncertainty and mental strain. It can reveal that a way of working saves keystrokes, but increases the need for control or weakens the understanding of one's own code, and turns this into concrete questions for improvement.

## Inputs and Evidence

Voluntary aggregate heart rate measurements, team input, interruption registration and representative qualitative comments.

## Prerequisites

Safe voluntary participation and routines that prevent recognition in small groups.

## Example and Deliverable

Developers must control many agent proposals. AI makes a theme summary of load, trust and lack of system understanding without personal names.

## Safeguards and Review

The team validates the interpretation and decides on measures. Do not make inferences about individual health, ability or productivity.

## Measuring Impact

Perceived friction and focus at team level, with response rate and representativeness as control measures.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
