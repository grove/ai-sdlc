# O06 Observe the usefulness of documents and decisions

<a id="o06"></a>

- **Stage:** [Observation](../stages/observation.md)
- **Category:** [Users and business outcomes](../stages/observation.md#o-category-1)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** Documents and Decisions, Regulated Systems, Legacy Systems

## Opportunity

AI can monitor whether an ADR, supervisor, test strategy or analysis is used in later work. It looks for traceable references and obtains assessments of decision usefulness, so that projects without running production code can also observe the effect of the deliverables.

## Inputs and Evidence

Approved document versions, references from tasks and decisions as well as feedback from recipients.

## Prerequisites

A named recipient and a concrete definition of what the document should make possible.

## Example and Deliverable

An architectural study has been delivered. AI creates a usage overview that shows which decisions it substantiated, and which questions still needed to be clarified.

## Safeguards and Review

Recipient confirms actual benefit. Downloads or citations alone are not evidence of better decisions.

## Measuring Impact

Time to clarified decision and registered rematches per comparable decision, with professional correctness and residual uncertainty.

## Applying This Idea

Link the measurement to the agreed hypothesis and actual exposure to the measure. Show data gaps, alternative explanations and uncertainty before an observed change is referred to as an effect.
