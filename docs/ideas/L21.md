# L21 Simplify collaboration between multiple agents

<a id="l21"></a>

- **Stage:** [Learning](../stages/learning.md)
- **Category:** [Improve agents and work tools](../stages/learning.md#l-category-3)
- **Operating Mode:** Advisory
- **Especially Relevant To:** AI in the product, Platform Engineering, Product Development

## Opportunity

AI can compare orchestration patterns and find work that is duplicated or lost between agents. It suggests clearer role boundaries or a simpler solution, so that coordination, conflicts and extra model calls do not overshadow the value of parallel work.

## Inputs and Evidence

Agent roles, handover contracts, driving logs, conflict detection, costs and acceptance criteria.

## Prerequisites

Traceable handovers and the possibility of comparative testing of a simpler way of working.

## Example and Deliverable

The deployment and test agents modify the same files. AI creates a new work sharing proposal with delimited folders and a controlled compilation port.

## Safeguards and Review

Technical owner evaluates against a simpler baseline. Multiple agents do not automatically provide independent reviews or better results.

## Measuring Impact

Total time and cost to approved delivery, with conflicting work, duplication and errors as control targets.

## Applying This Idea

Turn the learning into a concrete measure with owner and follow-up date. Keep negative findings and test the change before using it as a new standard or scaling it to more tasks.
